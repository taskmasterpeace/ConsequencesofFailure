# FIST / SPEAR — Vaughn Galloway's paramilitary  `[faction file]`

## CANON — the organization
- **SPEAR** = Galloway's **vision** (rejected): *Special Personnel for Extraterrestrial Assault & Research* — an **international** team where all nations unite their LSWs against the coming Alliance of Four. He proposes it at the UN; **the US, China, and Russia veto it.** Galloway calls the rejection **"the worst mistake in human history."** **SPEAR is never created.**
- **FIST** = the **reality** (US-only fallback): *First Infantry Strike Team* — a private paramilitary the White House asks Galloway to lead after the **Tragedy at Andrews** + the Living Super Weapon Threshold Treaty. Scientists + soldiers + LSWs. To the public, America's answer to the LSW threat; to Washington, just an arm of its intelligence/military.
- **Founder / Director:** **Vaughn Galloway** — non-powered eccentric billionaire, UFO enthusiast; **Charles Sapphire's** ex-best-friend/roommate and arch-rival.
- **Ops commander:** **Todd "Shogun" Benchley** — Black ex-Airborne Ranger Colonel, ex-**Akrahuhum** POW (Galloway paid his ransom). Leads the Military Division. *(Wife divorces him — drop the old "car-crash" draft.)*
- **Division heads** (from `SPEAR_… .txt`): **Dr. Evelyn Saunders** (Science), **Dr. Benjamin Franklin** (Engineering), **Cloudia Asaad** (Tactical Ops, ex-Mossad), **Dr. Jennifer Walters** (Medical).
- **Rival:** **Deck 52** (Charles Sapphire). The two PMCs are direct competitors; personal before professional.

5. **"The Supercomputer"** — Todd's team guards a black-site where a **supercomputer is learning human behavior in preparation for the war.** *(Hook: dovetails with the Greys' machine-learning selection AND Charles's secret AI — a thread to exploit.)*
6. **"The Alien"** — investigate a crashed craft on a Pacific island; first hostile alien contact; SPEAR's would-be role foreshadowed.
7. **"The Showdown" (finale pt 1)** — FIST's/Benchley's existence goes public under heavy scrutiny; a plan to hit the alien mothership is **thwarted by Deck 52**, who are working the same threat for their own gain.
8. **"The Final Battle" (finale pt 2)** — Todd's team must **team up with Deck 52** to take the mothership; victory at great cost; team losses; Todd left to rebuild. Cliffhanger.
