# THE GREYS — deep-time custodians of Earth  `[faction file — developed 2026-06-29]`

The aliens who turn ~1,000 humans into Living Super Weapons to try to save Earth. **Not the enemy — guilt-ridden allies.** Their whole motivation is a single tragic engine: **they keep breaking the worlds they touch, across deep time, and can't stop trying to atone.** (Sources: creator originals `canon/aliens/Zuma Rock.txt`, `canon/aliens/RADIO_WAVE_INCIDENT.md`; the 2026-06-28/29 sessions.)

## The deep-time spine — three silences, one careless hand
The Greys have operated in this solar system since **before humanity.** Their history is a chain of accidental extinctions, each deepening their guilt:

1. **Mars — the first silence.** The Greys' **first base** in this system was on Mars. An operation went wrong and **stripped Mars** — its air, water, magnetic dynamo — leaving the dead red husk we see today. *(Why Mars is barren = a Grey catastrophe. The exact cause is intentionally OPEN → the writers' room pitches it; the room's note: make it rhyme with their signature mistake so they're serial offenders with one flaw.)*
2. **The Long Burial — the dinosaur-killer.** Fleeing dead Mars, the Greys came to Earth — and **their arrival IS the Yucatán impact (Mexico) that ended the dinosaurs.** The "asteroid" is humanity's cover story; it was the Greys reaching their new refuge and scarring it the moment they arrived. **An accident** (matches Mars + the Silent Requiem — three honest mistakes = the cleanest guilt; keeps "no supervillains").
3. **Zuma Rock = the gateway.** They settled and built their **door** here — a stable wormhole anchored by Zuma's electromagnetic properties (canon). **Zuma is the gateway, full stop** — their Earth base and the network's hub. Nothing is buried under it. The **one active door** runs **Zuma ↔ the dead Mars base** (you can step from inside Zuma Rock to the silent Mars ruins — a future set-piece).
4. **The Reseeding.** Over ~66 million years, life **recovers** from the Long Burial — flows back across the planet — and eventually rises as **humanity, in Africa, right beside the Greys' gateway.** No "source of life," no magic: nature rebounds while the guilty landlords watch from Zuma. **That proximity is why the Greys have shepherded humans from the very start.**
5. **The Silent Requiem — the relapse.** Much later, humanity gets loud (radio), the Greys' gateway/teleport work lets the waves through, and an **energy-based species dies** (see below). The same mistake, a third time. → triggers the Concord, the quarantine, the Alliance, the 8-year clock.

**Net:** the Greys' motivation is **atonement across millions of years.** Alien, tragic, simple — and it makes turning 1,000 humans into weapons read as a desperate attempt to save the one world they keep failing.

## How they learned us (already canon)
The Greys can study human **biology** but not our **psychology**, so they outsourced understanding human behavior to a sentient alien **data-lifeform — "Its Voice."** That's how they chose the ~1,000. Its Voice's **origin species = "the Archive"** (a data/mind-collecting civilization; data is a cosmic delicacy, like energy). See `bible/factions/ITS_VOICE.md`.

## The sites that CONFIRM Rusty (the present-day payoff — dual use)
The deep-time sites aren't just backstory; they're the **evidence that validates the herald.** When Rusty Richards returns and says *they've been here since before us*, the world calls him a crackpot — until scientists, **now knowing what to look for**, find what was always there:
- **The Yucatán crater (Mexico)** — the impact/iridium layer that "proved" a natural asteroid reads differently once you know a Grey arrival-signature. *(Dual use: Mexico is already cartel turf — Cuatro Dedos, Jance, the Clown-Sheriff.)*
- **Göbekli Tepe (Turkey)** — 11,000 yrs old, deliberately buried by its builders, purpose unknown — its carvings re-read as a human **record** of the Greys. *(Dual use: Turkey is already Stefanos/Alliance geopolitics.)*

These two are **ancient, dormant traces — NOT active doors** (that's exactly why they're archaeology/proof, not portals — keeps the Greys' footprint small). The flip from "Rusty's insane" → "oh god, he's right" runs through them.
