# CUATRO DEDOS CARTEL  `[faction note — capture from "American Expansion" + "Condemned"]`

The Mexican cartel that supplies the grounded, **no-powers** crime layer of COF. It is the buyer at the far end of the Shooter's Club ghost-gun pipeline (`prose/shooters-club/CONDEMNED.md`) and the org **Jance Bloomberg** infiltrates and reforms. New treatment: `prose/cuatro-dedos/AMERICAN_EXPANSION.md` (the cartel's push into the U.S. — Montana).

## What the cartel is
- **Cuatro Dedos** ("Four Fingers") — a hands-off, professionalized cartel that runs fentanyl/meth (and, via the Shooter's Club, untraceable guns). Run with cold, systems-minded discipline; the boss "does not deal in details — he has people for details" (per Rafa in CONDEMNED).
- **Operating principle:** keep the people who actually touch the crime few and replaceable; quality climbs the ladder and draws the boss's attention; an asset never gets to quit. (This is the exact mechanism that swallows the Shooter's Club in CONDEMNED — "the appetite comes downstairs.")
- **Spelling:** treatments use **"Cuatro Dedos"**; the roster faction line uses **"Cuatros Dedos"** (via the Jance files). ⚠ Spelling not reconciled — see conflicts.

### The Montana expansion plan (C2)
- **Why Montana:** vast unmonitored land, thin/under-resourced law enforcement, isolation.
- **Target:** the **reservations** (overlooked, underprotected) — entry via jobs/"protection," i.e. recruitment + coercion.
- **Network:** build out **local dealers**; move on **back roads** with **four-wheel drives**.
- **Safe houses:** old farmhouses, abandoned mines.
- Diego's standing warning: **failure won't be forgiven.**

### U.S. / cop side (the protagonist of the Montana thread)
- **Officer Daniel "Dan" Torres** — small-town **Montana** cop. Patrols a county wracked by the drug scourge; badly under-resourced (three squad cars for the whole county). His brother **Michael** died of drugs (alley flashback); he's vowed to fight the trade — putting him on a collision course with Cuatro Dedos as it reaches Montana. Makes an early arrest of a dealer who taunts him ("Daniel Torres… you think this changes anything?").
