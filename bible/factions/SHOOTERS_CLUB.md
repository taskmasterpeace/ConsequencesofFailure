# THE SHOOTER'S CLUB  `[canon capture — from "Condemned"]`

The grounded, **no-powers** floor of the COF world: a prestige police-corruption tragedy. Story: `prose/shooters-club/CONDEMNED.md`.

## What "Condemned" establishes
- The **Shooter's Club** is an elite LAPD anti-gang unit (the *Times'* nickname) led by **Sgt. Eugene "Gene" Bonny**. Publicly the most effective gun-seizing unit in the city; secretly running a **ghost-gun skim** out of "the Range" (a sub-basement gunsmithing shop + furnace): they log seized guns as "unserviceable," melt only the serialized part for the state inspector, and **resurrect the untracked parts onto blank frames** → untraceable guns sold to the cartel.
- **Why Gene does it:** his daughter **Maddie's** catastrophic illness and its crushing medical debt. "The condemned arithmetic" — he'd make every move again.
- **Rob Holt** — Gene's protégé/surrogate son (haunted by a partner who bled out in "eleven minutes"). His tragedy: *loyalty to the man, not the plan.* Breaks the rules out of love, hands the unit to the cartel, kills informant **Eddie Pruitt**, and ultimately **wires up and flips** — but Gene takes the whole fall on tape to save him.
- **Dawn Petrakis** — IA sergeant who only hunts "guns the system swore were dead" (her brother was killed by one). The patient nemesis; goes *around* the chain of command to a federal prosecutor (**Linda Soh**).
- **Chief Vernon Acker** — clean for 30 years until he buries his son **Tyler's** ghost-gun arrest; Gene leverages that ("the corner is the safest place in the room").
- **The buyers:** **Rafa Morales** → the **Cuatro Dedos** cartel → **Diego Rivera ("El Maestro").** The cartel "appetite coming downstairs" is the engine of collapse.
