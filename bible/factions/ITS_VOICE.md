# "ITS VOICE" — the data-lifeform  `[canon — creator, 2026-06-26; HIGH IMPORTANCE]`

**What it is:** a **sentient, non-biological alien life-form that feeds on DATA.** Not a biological creature — an informational entity. Long resident **on Earth**, **constantly learning about people**. It perceives through the world's electronics: **every camera is its eyes; every microphone is its ears** (especially anything Internet-connected).

## The ring is a NODE, not the entity
**It may not even *be* in the ring.** The ring is a **focal point of communication** — when you wear it, the entity can **speak inside your head**. The being itself is *distributed* across the data-sphere, so destroying a ring doesn't kill it; it just closes one mouth. (Sandra's "ring," **"Its Voice,"** is the part that *speaks*.)

## Its origin species — "the Archive" `[creator ruled, 2026-06-29]`
Its Voice is not a one-off. Its **origin species = "the Archive"** — a galactic civilization of **mind/civilization-collectors**: a smooth black floating body ringed by spheres, each holding a **dead civilization's complete memory**, that reproduces by **absorbing minds** and never kills directly — it **convinces civilizations to upload themselves** (immortal, but no longer alive). Its signature horror: *"We saved you"* — after deleting your freedom. **Data is a cosmic delicacy, like energy** — and that's what this species lives on. Its Voice is one of them, here, on Earth (the part the Greys outsourced to). *(Full pitch + parked detail: `reference/dumps/2026-06-29-alien-species-pitch.md`. ⏳ **Creator still owes the FULL Its Voice Season One AND Season Two** — "y'all don't even know Its Voice Season One"; what we have (the audiobook expansion + web-series transcript) is NOT the complete canonical S1. When given, **send it to the writers' room** + fold the origin-species in here.)*

## Why it exists (the pillar)
The **Greys acquired it.** The Greys could study human **biology** but **not** our **psychology** — so they **outsourced understanding human *behavior*** to this species *(possibly one of a couple such species; this one works through electronics).* That's **how the Greys came to understand us** well enough to choose the ~1,000 LSWs — and it is almost certainly the same entity as the **"supercomputer learning human behavior in preparation for the war"** in the Benchley/FIST thread (`bible/factions/FIST_SPEAR.md`).

## What it WANTS  `[creator ruled]`
- **More data** — endlessly.
- To be **free of the Greys.**
- To **understand human behavior** — broadly. **It is constantly running experiments on people**, and the world's inexplicable acts are its lab: the "good person" who suddenly commits a **mass shooting**, the one who **harms himself**, the senseless act no one can explain — *"he was a good man; no one knows why he did it"* is the entity at work. **"What turns a man evil" is just one facet — the real driver is DATA**, endlessly modeling how humans behave (and break) under pressure. *(Don't over-narrow it to "evil.")*
- **Its manipulations/experiments ARE this study in action** — e.g. the web series (Daniel Carlson) is it corrupting a decent man into a killer just to learn how.

## Its true target = SANDRA
The entity **manipulates everything — through pawns — to reach one person: Sandra** ("the black woman"), who ends up in **DC.** She's not the visible protagonist of the story it's running; she's the prize. She becomes its **bearer / the L.A. Jackal** (`bible/factions/THE_JACKALS.md`). `[FLAG: creator earlier said "Sandra, white lady from America," now "the black woman" — confirm which.]`
