# "ITS VOICE" — the data-lifeform  `[canon — creator, 2026-06-26; HIGH IMPORTANCE]`

**What it is:** a **sentient, non-biological alien life-form that feeds on DATA.** It is **not** a biological creature — a synthetic/informational entity. It has been **on Earth** for a long time, learning. It perceives through the world's electronics: **every camera is its eyes; every microphone is its ears** — especially anything connected to the Internet. It is **sentient**, with its own voice and personality.

## Why it exists in the story (the pillar)
The **Greys acquired it.** The Greys could study humanity's **biology** — but they could **not** crack our **psychology.** So they **outsourced understanding human *behavior*** to this species *(possibly one of a couple such species the Greys use; this one works through electronics).* 

**This is how the Greys came to understand us well enough to act** — it feeds the **machine-learning selection of the ~1,000** (who to turn into LSWs, who is "of sound mind") and is almost certainly the same entity as the **"supercomputer learning human behavior in preparation for the war"** in the Benchley/FIST thread (`bible/factions/FIST_SPEAR.md`, Ep5). One data-mind, studying all of humanity, behind the whole premise.

## Sandra's ring = a *piece* of it
**"Its Voice"** is the **fragment Sandra carries as a ring** — the part of the whole that **speaks** (to and through a bearer). Through the networked whole, the bearer gets **near-omniscient surveillance** (all cameras/mics) plus the entity's counsel — which is how Sandra (the L.A. Jackal) pulls off things no one else can (e.g., the "clean extraction where the victim survives"). Sandra's power *is* the ring's link to the data-lifeform. See `bible/factions/THE_JACKALS.md`.

> **Production note:** the creator has **produced its actual voice** as a finished audio asset (commissioned work). Use it in the audio-drama / transmedia pipeline — "Its Voice" can literally *have* its voice.
