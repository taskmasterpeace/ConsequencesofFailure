## CANON — the organization
- **Establishment 24** = India's UN-compliant private LSW establishment, **closely aligned with the government** — effectively an arm of India's military and intelligence (parallels Deck 52 / FIST as the national LSW org).
- **Won worldwide acclaim for fixing India's physical infrastructure** — electricity, railways, roads, ports, airports, irrigation, water supply, sanitation (all previously in deficit). Held up as **the framework for how a country uses its LSW citizens to improve life for everyone** — a surface utopia (infrastructure, housing, social disputes resolved).
- **Icchatām Ganga** ("The Wishing Ganga") — its most ambitious project: extracting all impurities from the Ganga. Per the original, it **removed ~80% of the river's waste and pollutants.**
- **Mission/vision (original):** make India safe and prosperous for all Indians by any means necessary; establish India as a leading world power by using LSW citizens to their fullest while keeping peace at home. Core values: Patriotism, Dedication, Sacrifice.
- **Missions come exclusively from the government of India.** Private parties cannot hire E24. Funded by and given wide operational leeway by the state.
- **Specialty divisions (original):** assassination squad, data-&-analytics division (war-gaming past conflicts into best practices — credited with Kargil War intel), drone division.
- **Secrets (original):** credited with high-profile assassinations (Paresh Baruah, Dawood Ibrahim); large but unknown number of LSW citizens on staff (hundreds of members total).
- **Stated means (original):** *not* willing to violate international law or harm innocents — willing to partner with other countries/orgs. **TENSION:** this directly collides with the brain-dump's militarization-pressure arc and the assassination secrets above (see "To develop").

## CANON — leadership & key people
- **Colonel Raghavan Reddy** — founder/leader. Strong, charismatic, die-hard patriot, no-nonsense; INFJ; brings out the best in people, brave, protective, wise, **overly cautious, values his team's lives over his own.** One of the greatest colonels of his generation (origin lore says ~1,000 confirmed kills before he left the army seeking peace at Kedarnath).
  - **Power — Divine Metamorphosis (LSW Lv5):** an extremely durable **golden plastic/wax-like alien lifeform** bonds to his skin as living external armor + wings; lets him morph into a hybrid **man-eagle "Garuda"** form (super strength, sharp senses, predatory instinct, flight, sometimes **4 golden wings**, talons + beak). The substance can **detect and transmit emotion**, heals/sedates him, and forms constructs (blades, tendrils, handholds). **Weakness (per `COF_India.txt` Scripts):** loses power when submerged in water. *(Note: the Scripts block also gives an alt "wax-controlling CEO from Hyderabad" version of Reddy — treat the Garuda/Greys origin as the trusted line; flag the CEO variant as a discarded draft.)*
  - **Origin (per `COF_India.txt`):** survives a **bus accident** in the Himalayas that kills his family; at the moment of death he is **pulled into a swirling vortex**, vanishes ~14 days, and reappears DNA-fused with a Grey-engineered aerial lifeform. The living armor says it was deployed because **a war is coming in ~8 years** and India needs a defender; it tells him to ask the Chief Defense Officer for details.  ✅ **RULED 2026-06-26: this vortex IS the one recurring vortex of the whole universe** — the Greys' displacement/abduction phenomenon, the same vortex everywhere (including Reddy's origin). See RESOLVED note below.
- **Major Gupta** — Reddy's right-hand man / #2 (original org chart: Reddy + Gupta over a team of 12 agents).
- **Asha** — time-traveler, 22, college student from **Kozhikode** (LSW Lv5 on the **LeFevre Origin Threat Scale**). Travels weeks into the future; snaps back when she touches a person/anything that affects time; each trip costs migraines, disorientation, memory loss, emotional instability, physical weakening, paranoia. Travels too far → becomes invisible to the present. Two near-identical mongoose pets (a side-effect of her power). Recruited by Reddy. **One of only two known time travelers in COF** (the other = Zhang Wei, China — a past-traveler).

## CANON-ADJACENT — government & framing (from `COF_India.txt`, trusted)
- **Vijay Randhawa** — **Chief Defense Officer of India**; the government's liaison who architects E24. Tall, slender, grey-haired, chain-smoker, calculating; ~3 decades in the Ministry of Defense. He recognizes Reddy in the viral Kedarnath flood-rescue video and pushes to stand up the establishment. Per the **UN Superhero/Supers Threshold Treaty**, the establishment's head **cannot be government-affiliated** — which is why Randhawa stays liaison and **Reddy is made the (retroactively discharged) figurehead leader.**
- **Vikram Patel** — Defense Minister; runs the covert program; first to surveil Asha; debates the ethics of recruiting Reddy.
- **UN context:** the Supers Threshold Treaty caps how many LSWs a country may employ *directly*, forcing nations to route them through arm's-length private establishments (USA → Deck 52 / billionaire; China → its TV show; India → Establishment 24). Reddy's discharge is the legal fiction that makes E24 treaty-compliant.

## The militarization conflict (the live dramatic engine — brain-dump)
The government leans on Reddy to **wield E24 as a military extension against adversaries abroad** — **illegal under the treaty, but the UN cannot enforce it.** Reddy is torn: a loyal soldier, but militarizing the establishment means the **infrastructure and social-good LSWs get spent (and lost) in combat**, betraying the very people E24 was built to serve. *(This is the cleanest no-plot-armor stakes for the India thread: every LSW pulled to the front is one fewer keeping the lights on.)*
