## CANON — the organization
- **Establishment 24** = India's UN-compliant private LSW establishment, **closely aligned with the government** — effectively an arm of India's military and intelligence (parallels Deck 52 / FIST as the national LSW org).
- **Won worldwide acclaim for fixing India's physical infrastructure** — electricity, railways, roads, ports, airports, irrigation, water supply, sanitation (all previously in deficit). Held up as **the framework for how a country uses its LSW citizens to improve life for everyone** — a surface utopia (infrastructure, housing, social disputes resolved).
- **Icchatām Ganga** ("The Wishing Ganga") — its most ambitious project: extracting all impurities from the Ganga. Per the original, it **removed ~80% of the river's waste and pollutants.**
- **Mission/vision (original):** make India safe and prosperous for all Indians by any means necessary; establish India as a leading world power by using LSW citizens to their fullest while keeping peace at home. Core values: Patriotism, Dedication, Sacrifice.
- **Missions come exclusively from the government of India.** Private parties cannot hire E24. Funded by and given wide operational leeway by the state.
- **Specialty divisions (original):** assassination squad, data-&-analytics division (war-gaming past conflicts into best practices — credited with Kargil War intel), drone division.
- **Secrets (original):** credited with high-profile assassinations (Paresh Baruah, Dawood Ibrahim); large but unknown number of LSW citizens on staff (hundreds of members total).
- **Stated means (original):** *not* willing to violate international law or harm innocents — willing to partner with other countries/orgs. **TENSION:** this directly collides with the brain-dump's militarization-pressure arc and the assassination secrets above (see "To develop").
