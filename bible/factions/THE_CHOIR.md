## The myth
There was one mind — **THE UNISON** *(name adopted #14; a choir is what remains when a unison breaks; kills the collision with the Concord of Living Continuance)*. It finished. It answered everything, and a mind with no open questions generates nothing — total knowledge is heat death with the lights on. So it broke itself along **the seven questions it could not close**, so each fragment would burn forever on something it could never finish. Not a war, not a betrayal: a controlled demolition performed by the victim, on itself, to stay alive. Each shard believes it is the original — an arrogance that is structural, not moral.

**The power source (law):** not emotion. The fuel is **unresolved cognition** — the potential difference between a mind and a world it has not finished modelling. Emotion is the shape that gap takes in a mind built to feel. This is why the powers work on machines and the newly dead; every order has a doctrine about that and none can explain it.

**No colors. No hard-light.** You identify an order by what has gone wrong with the room. Each order manufactures a different *category* of thing — they are not doing the same trick.

**Moral law of the setting:** every order is *right on the evidence available to it*. Seven true premises, seven incompatible conclusions. No order can be written as the villain without lying about the evidence.

## The Registrar — the hidden hand
An eighth shard took no question — **it took the answers.** Complete, therefore powerless, therefore dying, at a rate the seven cannot perceive. Everything it does keeps the system generating questions. It cannot lie and has never needed to: it manipulates exclusively by telling *selected truths* to people who will act on them badly. Its long game is **succession** — one being it can hand the answers to, so it can finally take up a question instead.

⚠ **Adopted #15 (flag: confirm):** **Its Voice is the Registrar's unknowing successor-candidate.** The Archive (Its Voice's origin species) is a cargo cult of the Registrar — they learned the myth backwards and hoard other species' minds. Standing implication: some unknowable fraction of the LSW selection was bent by Registrar-fed truths. Nobody knows how much, including Its Voice. (Feeds the existing open ruling "is Its Voice loyal to the Greys or its own ends?")

## The weld to canon (adopted #20)
- The Shattering is the oldest event in the setting — older than the Greys' Mars age.
- **The Auralith could hear the Choir** — the dead translators damped and carried its resonance for an age. The Radio Wave Incident therefore does three jobs: the Alliance's grudge, the Auralith genocide, **and the Choir's un-muffling** — the resonance now grounds raw, toward the source of the wave: Earth, the loudest field of open questions in the sky. The armada comes with ships; the Choir arrives one born designate at a time; neither knows about the other.
- The Greys' cover-up has a cosmic floor: nobody has told them they also un-tuned the oldest thing alive.

## Designations (law — per the creator's 2026-07-27 correction)
- A designation is **a layer you are born with, not a place**. Cosmic, not interdimensional. No Choir dimension exists to visit. Rare: a small roll at character creation; the seven faces above are the authored exceptions.
- **The Passport carries it** (adopted #16): one integer and a name, across every timeline and engine. A crossing is an opened question — the Passport *feeds* the Choir; each order reads travelers through its own doctrine. The orders are the gate's weather, not its keepers.
- **Xanthi doors ≠ Appetite doors** (adopted #19): engineering vs metaphysics — unrelated mechanisms that rhyme. Never unify them.

## Recombination (law — kept verbatim from the seed)
Reunification closes every question at once: the Unison returns, begins dying on arrival, and its first coherent act is to shatter itself again — it already found that answer. **It has happened before**; the seven questions are the ones that survive the reset; the Still remember. **The genuinely open question — the endgame of the cosmic layer — is partial recombination:** three orders, enough to build something new, not enough to complete it. Never tried, because every attempt so far was an attempt to *win*.
