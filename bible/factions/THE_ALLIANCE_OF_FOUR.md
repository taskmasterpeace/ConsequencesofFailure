# THE ALLIANCE OF FOUR — the coming threat  `[faction file]`

The militarized armada bound for Earth — the war the whole series counts down to (full arrival ~Season 7–8). Humanity has **~8 years.**

**Origin of the grudge `[refined — creator, 2026-06-28 dump]`:** **radio waves from Earth** rode in with the **Greys** — as the Greys tore through space to travel, the radio waves **contaminated their ship** — and **decimated an energy-based species** on the far side of the universe: **complete genocide of a very important species.** Radio waves are lethal to energy-based life (like rain to a thing that can't be rained on). The Greys **realized what they had done and decided to COVER IT UP** — to hide that they had anything to do with it. It became agreed across the universe that **"it all came from Earth."** Because radio waves crawl across space for ages and **humanity is a loud radio-broadcasting world**, the death is pinned on Earth — and the Greys let it be, because the alternative is exposing their own role. The aliens even **rename Earth** (they don't call it "Earth") `[new name TBD]`. *(Cross-ref `RADIO_WAVE_INCIDENT.md`.)* `[open: how a council communicates faster-than-light across space.]`

**The split `[creator]`.** Once the universe agreed Earth was responsible, opinion divided into two camps:
- **Let it go / bring Earth into the circle** — it was a mistake; admit Earth to the interstellar community. *(The doctrine treats this side as naive — a mistake.)*
- **Smash Earth** — these are **chronic polluters**: they fouled their own planet, and have now found a way to **pollute across space and time** (the radio waves), before even reaching the interstellar stage everyone was waiting for. They must be put down.

The second camp becomes the **Alliance of Four** — nominally four species, but in truth **one group that gathered up other species** under its banner.

**The Strategist's doctrine `[creator]`.** The Alliance's leader/strategist holds that **the war started the moment that species died** — not when it was formally declared. The strategy follows from that: **do not arrive as four species over Earth and fight it.** Instead, **weaken Earth first — bring it to its knees** — so that by the time the Alliance arrives, the planet is already broken and they simply **set up shop.**

## The Greys — allies (NOT one of the Four)
Guilt-driven (and strategic) dimensional travelers who try to prepare us: they abduct ~**1,000** people of sound mind and return them transformed into **Living Super Weapons (LSWs)**, betting humanity will unite. We didn't — we turned the gifts on each other. The Greys misjudged us.

**How they learned us:** the Greys could study human *biology* but **not** our *psychology* — so they **outsourced understanding human behavior** to a sentient alien **data-lifeform** ("**Its Voice**" — every camera its eyes, every mic its ears). That's how they came to know us well enough to choose the ~1,000 (and it's almost certainly the "supercomputer learning human behavior" in the FIST thread). See `bible/factions/ITS_VOICE.md`.

Self-evolving, metal-eating nanite swarm/AI. The **"softener" wave sent ahead of the main armada** — the advance force any smart invasion deploys first, the instrument of the Strategist's "bring Earth to its knees first" doctrine: consume infrastructure, weaken the defenders. **Deck 52** captures and weaponizes them into **RoboForge v2**. (See `bible/factions/DECK_52_SAPPHIRE_GALLOWAY.md`.)

**How they were delivered `[new — creator, 2026-06-28 dump]`.** The Iron Eaters rode in as **"dust" the Alliance secretly planted on the GREYS' OWN ship.** The Greys, traveling to and from Earth, **unwittingly carried the dust here themselves** — it arrived via a **crashing satellite that came down in South America**. Investigators find **metal-eating, self-replicating nanites** (too small to see well) using the satellite's metal to make more of themselves; they devastate South America (a Mad-Max apocalypse) while countries debate whether to help. Over time the Eaters evolve: nanites → small crawling forms → **big junk-metal robots** tearing through buildings, eating the metal in your house (the fridge, the pipes) bit by bit.

**Why the Greys panic `[new — creator]`.** The method itself is the threat to them. If the **Alliance** was able to plant the dust on the **Greys' own ship**, then the Alliance may have been **close enough to the Greys to know the Greys caused the original genocide.** Their cover-up — "it all came from Earth" — is suddenly at risk; the Greys look untrustworthy and exposed. They mostly let the invasion play out rather than tip their hand. *(An AI/time-traveler from the future warns it won't work out; many don't believe him.)*

## THE XANTHI — the Doorwalkers  `[PROPOSED — creator to bless/tune]`
The Alliance's **infiltration & decapitation arm.** Where the Iron Eaters soften and the Strategists plan, the Xanthi simply **arrive** — anywhere, with no landing, no front line.

**What they do.** They fold space and open **dimensional doors**, stepping through body-first. They can also cast their **perception** — sight and hearing — through these apertures to *witness* almost anywhere in existence. Functionally: near-omniscient observers and nearly unstoppable infiltrators.

**The Lock — why they aren't omnipotent (the rule that makes them beatable).** A Xanthi can't open a door to a place it has no **lock** on. Space never holds still — Earth spins, orbits the sun; the sun drifts through the galaxy — so a "location" is a constantly-moving coordinate. Travel is really *solving that moving equation for a target you're anchored to.* No anchor, no door. *(This is the kind of in-world "contact science" the series likes to explain plainly — see CLAUDE.md, the insight layer.)*

**The Mark — the slow-burn horror (the "mind virus").** When a Xanthi has been **near a person**, it plants a latent **anchor** in them — a sub-perceptual infection. From then on, **wherever that person goes, a Xanthi can open a door to them.** You become a permanent doorway the enemy can always find — and you **carry the breach to everyone around you.** The world doesn't understand at first *why* the Xanthi keep appearing exactly where key people are; the reveal — *proximity is permanent exposure* — lands late and changes everything. (An infected diplomat is a walking war-room breach. Do you quarantine the marked? Exile your own leaders?)

**Limits (no plot armor).**
- **Bodies only** — a Xanthi (and possibly **one allied race** they ferry through the doors) can send a *body* through; they can't reach through to teleport a bomb or a fleet. The threat is infiltration, not instant annihilation.
- **Anchors, not addresses** — without an anchor, a place/person is invisible to them. The unmarked can't be reached.
- **The counter-play (how you stop them):** not brute force — **sever or cloak the anchor.** Breaking a Xanthi's lock on a marked person is a research holy-grail — a job for **Karine/NuroNuro** (brain/signal nano-tech), a **Sage** of the right discipline, or **Cissy's** genetics to scramble the anchor's signature. `[OPEN: does the anchor fade with time/distance, or stay until severed? Recommend permanent-until-severed for max dread.]`

**Why it's good story.** It turns *who you've stood next to* into a weapon, forces impossible quarantine/loyalty choices, and gives a terrifying enemy a clean, fair, discoverable rule the heroes can fight — exactly the no-plot-armor shape.
