# THE ALLIANCE OF FOUR — the coming threat  `[faction file]`

The militarized armada bound for Earth — the war the whole series counts down to (full arrival ~Season 7–8). Humanity has **~8 years.**

**Origin of the grudge `[refined — creator]`:** the **Greys'** constant **jumping/travel** between here and "there" throws off **radio waves**, which are **lethal to energy-based life** (like rain to a thing that can't be rained on) — and one such wave **decimated an entire energy-based species.** Because radio waves crawl across space for ages and **humanity is a loud radio-broadcasting world**, the death gets **pinned on Earth** — whether the responsible Greys genuinely misattribute it or **frame us to bury their own role** `[open]`. The aliens even **rename Earth** (they don't call it "Earth") `[new name TBD]`. An **intergalactic council** convenes to decide what to do; once it rules **Earth responsible**, four militarized civilizations form the Alliance to come stop Earth from "polluting space." The Greys — guilty, and "over this part of Earth" — quietly try to prepare us. *(Cross-ref `RADIO_WAVE_INCIDENT.md`.)* `[open: how a council communicates faster-than-light across space.]`

## The Greys — allies (NOT one of the Four)
Guilt-driven (and strategic) dimensional travelers who try to prepare us: they abduct ~**1,000** people of sound mind and return them transformed into **Living Super Weapons (LSWs)**, betting humanity will unite. We didn't — we turned the gifts on each other. The Greys misjudged us.

**How they learned us:** the Greys could study human *biology* but **not** our *psychology* — so they **outsourced understanding human behavior** to a sentient alien **data-lifeform** ("**Its Voice**" — every camera its eyes, every mic its ears). That's how they came to know us well enough to choose the ~1,000 (and it's almost certainly the "supercomputer learning human behavior" in the FIST thread). See `bible/factions/ITS_VOICE.md`.

## The precursor — the Iron Eaters (NOT one of the Four)
Self-evolving, metal-eating nanite swarm/AI. **A scout / "softener" wave sent ahead of the main armada (~Year 4)** — the advance force any smart invasion deploys first: consume infrastructure, weaken the defenders. **Deck 52** captures and weaponizes them into **RoboForge v2**. (See `bible/factions/DECK_52_SAPPHIRE_GALLOWAY.md`.)

## THE XANTHI — the Doorwalkers  `[PROPOSED — creator to bless/tune]`
The Alliance's **infiltration & decapitation arm.** Where the Iron Eaters soften and the Strategists plan, the Xanthi simply **arrive** — anywhere, with no landing, no front line.

**What they do.** They fold space and open **dimensional doors**, stepping through body-first. They can also cast their **perception** — sight and hearing — through these apertures to *witness* almost anywhere in existence. Functionally: near-omniscient observers and nearly unstoppable infiltrators.

**The Lock — why they aren't omnipotent (the rule that makes them beatable).** A Xanthi can't open a door to a place it has no **lock** on. Space never holds still — Earth spins, orbits the sun; the sun drifts through the galaxy — so a "location" is a constantly-moving coordinate. Travel is really *solving that moving equation for a target you're anchored to.* No anchor, no door. *(This is the kind of in-world "contact science" the series likes to explain plainly — see CLAUDE.md, the insight layer.)*

**The Mark — the slow-burn horror (the "mind virus").** When a Xanthi has been **near a person**, it plants a latent **anchor** in them — a sub-perceptual infection. From then on, **wherever that person goes, a Xanthi can open a door to them.** You become a permanent doorway the enemy can always find — and you **carry the breach to everyone around you.** The world doesn't understand at first *why* the Xanthi keep appearing exactly where key people are; the reveal — *proximity is permanent exposure* — lands late and changes everything. (An infected diplomat is a walking war-room breach. Do you quarantine the marked? Exile your own leaders?)

**Limits (no plot armor).**
- **Bodies only** — a Xanthi (and possibly **one allied race** they ferry through the doors) can send a *body* through; they can't reach through to teleport a bomb or a fleet. The threat is infiltration, not instant annihilation.
- **Anchors, not addresses** — without an anchor, a place/person is invisible to them. The unmarked can't be reached.
- **The counter-play (how you stop them):** not brute force — **sever or cloak the anchor.** Breaking a Xanthi's lock on a marked person is a research holy-grail — a job for **Karine/NuroNuro** (brain/signal nano-tech), a **Sage** of the right discipline, or **Cissy's** genetics to scramble the anchor's signature. `[OPEN: does the anchor fade with time/distance, or stay until severed? Recommend permanent-until-severed for max dread.]`

**Why it's good story.** It turns *who you've stood next to* into a weapon, forces impossible quarantine/loyalty choices, and gives a terrifying enemy a clean, fair, discoverable rule the heroes can fight — exactly the no-plot-armor shape.
