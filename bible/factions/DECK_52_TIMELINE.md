# DECK 52 / SAPPHIRE–GALLOWAY — TIMELINE `[PROPOSED]`

How the corporate saga slots into the COF master clock (the **8-year countdown** to the Alliance of Four; **Year 1** = Rusty's warning + the first LSWs). The Sapphire–Galloway story is **pre-history that runs into the main timeline** — both PMCs are already entrenched when the powers arrive. Year-gaps before Year 1 are approximate `[PROPOSED]` — adjust freely.

## Pre-history (before the powers)
| When (approx.) | Beat |
|---|---|
| ~Yr −18 | Charles & Vaughn meet at **MIT**; become best friends. |
| ~Yr −12 | They found **Taskmaster**; both get rich. Charles meets **Emily Rhaige** (secretly engaged). |
| ~Yr −9 | Vaughn ships **Cheat Check** behind Charles's back — the **first fracture.** |
| ~Yr −8 | **Benchley POW → the ransom stunt** ($1.5M), engineered to humiliate **President Williamson** → Williamson pushed out → **Dr. Anton Parker** wins easily. The money funds **Akrahahum.** |
| ~Yr −7 | **Akrahahum murders Emily.** The **drunken interview.** The friendship breaks for good. |
| ~Yr −7 → −6 | Charles founds **Deck 52** + **Rhaige Sapphire**; Vaughn founds **FIST** (Benchley commanding). Two rival empires. |
| ~Yr −5 → −2 | **RoboForge v1** — Charles drives Cho to build it; a commercial juggernaut (→ billionaire) but **battlefield-useless** (power-source problem). The PMC arms race hardens; the **Wildcard Initiative** quietly begins. |

## Into the main clock
| When | Beat |
|---|---|
| **Year 1 — The Warning** | Rusty returns; the first LSWs appear; the world tilts. Deck 52 & FIST are *already* the private-army layer the new era inherits. **The burned woman → the Navy contract:** FIST beats Deck 52; Vaughn's ET obsession goes operational; **FIST → SPEAR.** |
| **Years 2–3 — the Hunt / proxy war** | The rivalry plays out inside the LSW scramble (Jackals, treaty-dodging, proxy ops). The **Wildcard Initiative** ramps — augmentation as Deck 52's edge; **Vance vs Cho** sharpens. |
| **Year 4 — Iron Eaters arrive early** | The breakthrough: Cho/Spades fuses captured **Iron Eater nanites** into RoboForge → **RoboForge v2** (self-sustaining, lethal). Deck 52 becomes a superpower — and is accused of *collaborating with the invaders.* |
| **Years 5–8 — endgame** | RoboForge v2 + the Wildcards make Deck 52 terrifying; **Charles declines** and the unspoken **succession war** boils (Pierce / Kané / Cho / Vance); the FIST–SPEAR rivalry escalates into the alien war. |

## The fixed relationships (everything else is adjustable)
- The **split** and **both PMCs** predate Year 1.
- **RoboForge v1 predates the aliens; v2 is born from them (Yr 4–5).**
- The **burned-woman contract sits at Year 1** — the hinge where the corporate saga meets the alien one.
