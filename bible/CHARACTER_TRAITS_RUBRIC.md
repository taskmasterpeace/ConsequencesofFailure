# CHARACTER TRAITS RUBRIC — the Edelstein method  `[bible tool]`

**Why:** the creator's "character traits = secret unlock." Flat hero-types are how the AI-rewrite layer failed. Every **SOLID** character gets a **Trait Sheet** so they read as a real, consistent individual — and so we can predict how they behave (and break) under pressure. Built from **Linda Edelstein, *Writer's Guide to Character Traits*** (Internal/Interpersonal trait clusters; the normal→pathological continuum). This becomes a section of each character note **and** a block in the character bible sheet.

## The principle (Edelstein, Ch.1)
- **Cohesiveness & consistency** — certain elements run through the *whole* character even when some traits are hidden.
- **Traits ↔ roles ↔ situations ↔ relationships** — each shapes the others; the same person shows different traits in different rooms.
- **Normal → Extreme continuum** — a strength deteriorates into its dark version when pushed. *(This is psychological no-plot-armor: it tells you how they crack.)*
- **Hint, don't dump** — show two or three traits, imply the rest. A character is always *more* than the list.
- **The "other side"** — one trait *against* type is what makes them human.

## The COF Trait Sheet (apply to every character)
1. **Core Type(s)** — pick **1–2** of Edelstein's 23 adult types (Ch.2) as the spine. Dark characters also draw on **Disorders (Ch.4)**, **Criminal Types (Ch.5)**, **Gangs/Cults (Ch.12)**.
2. **Internal** — emotion & attitude: drives, fears, self-image, what they believe.
3. **Interpersonal** — behavior toward allies / enemies / strangers / authority.
4. **Normal → Extreme** — which strengths, and what they curdle into when pushed.
5. **The Wound & the Drive** — the formative injury + the thing they rework their whole life.
6. **History** — the backstory that grew the traits.
7. **The Contradiction** — the against-type trait that makes them human.
8. **Tells** — body language, speech pattern, mannerisms, external attributes (Ch.13).
9. **Competence** — their career/training and how it shaped them (Ch.11).

**Type menu (Edelstein Ch.2):** Adventurer · Boss · Conformist · Conventional · Dependent · Eccentric · Flamboyant · Loner · Man's-Man · Passive-Aggressive · Victim · Introvert · Caretaker/Healer · Narcissist · Obsessive · Suspicious(Paranoid) · Hypersensitive … (23 total in the book). Darker spines live in Ch.4–5.

## EXAMPLE 1 — John Rivers "Stampede"
**Core Type(s):** **Loner** + a controlled **Boss** (aggressive energy under iron discipline) + trauma (Ch.4 complicated grief / PTSD). **Explicitly NOT the "Victim" type** — creator-ruled: a *cold* antihero, not a "poor-me / I-deserve-better" revenge arc.
- **Internal:** self-contained, walled-off, mistrustful of institutions; controlled to the point of cold; carries guilt about Rebecca he never voices; certainty that the system will never deliver justice, so he will.
- **Interpersonal:** terse, doesn't ask twice, unsettlingly calm; treats authority with quiet contempt; keeps everyone at arm's length; intimidating without raising his voice.
- **Normal → Extreme:** discipline → **ruthlessness** · control → **the "lose it" state** where he's a danger to *everyone*, not just the guilty (matches the menacing collapse-field) · self-reliance → total isolation.
- **Wound & Drive:** his sister **Det. Rebecca Carranza**'s murder *and* the Shooter's Club cover-up. He doesn't *advertise* grief — he **weaponizes** it.
- **Contradiction:** the cold killer keeps one private tenderness / one line he won't cross — the last human thing the rage hasn't eaten.
- **Tells:** stillness, economy of motion; goes *quiet* right before violence; **reads a room like a cop** (dovetails with his power's "must know the space" lock).
- **Competence:** LAPD training — procedure, threat assessment, reading people.

## EXAMPLE 2 — Kaiser Eziobi
**Core Type(s):** **Conventional/Conscientious** + **Caretaker/Healer**, with elder-statesman gravitas.
- **Internal:** dutiful, weary, deeply principled; **haunted** (he literally carries the memories of the dead); humble; an empathy so large it's a burden.
- **Interpersonal:** paternal, diplomatic, a unifier; slow to anger; listens far more than he speaks; commands moral authority rather than force.
- **Normal → Extreme:** empathy → **being crushed by others' grief** (the literal cost of the Memory Vessel) · conviction → **martyrdom** · patience → passivity while the powerful move against him.
- **Wound & Drive:** betrayal by his business partner; S.A.R.C. and exile; the weight of every dead person he holds — channeled into a Pan-African dignity dream.
- **Contradiction:** the gentle peacemaker is *exactly* the man foreign powers feel they must assassinate — **his softness is the threat.**
- **Tells:** measured speech; the physical toll of the memories shows on him; a stillness that reads as wisdom, not coldness.
- **Competence:** helicopter pilot — calm under pressure, precision, the long view from above.
