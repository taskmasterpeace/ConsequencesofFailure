## Identity  `[creator-locked]`
- **Name:** **Todd "Shogun" Benchley.** *(Earlier draft name "Todd **Ravage** Benchley" is SUPERSEDED — see conflicts.)*
- **Nationality:** **USA.** Born and raised in a tough neighborhood; father a garbage collector, stay-at-home mother.
- **Age / look:** ~54. Black, 5'9", slim-muscular ~190 lbs; light-brown skin. Rarely seen without his **trademark black cowboy hat** and his **black M16 with a suppressor**; cigar smoker. A man of few words.
- **Career:** Joined the Army at 18 → **Airborne Ranger** → 20 years' service, rose to **Colonel**, decorated for bravery. Saw action in Afghanistan, Iraq, Somalia. After the Army: firefighter, private investigator, bodyguard (for Galloway) → **private military contractor**.
- **Skills:** close-quarters & hand-to-hand combat, weapons of all kinds; **Eskrima** (Filipino martial art), **Judo black belt**; speaks German, Spanish, Tagalog.
- **Wound he carries:** haunted by his time as a **prisoner of war** (his biggest secret). Greatest fear: losing someone he loves. Greatest desire: to find a place where he belongs.
- **Archetype:** The Warrior / The Protector / The Mentor. Fiercely loyal, brave, disciplined, precise — and isolates himself by drowning in work.

## Capture origin — RULED 2026-06-26  `[LOCKED]`
**Locked spine (canon):** While serving in **Afghanistan**, Todd was **betrayed by a translator** and **captured by the Akrahuhum**, who held and tortured him for **months** as a POW. The story hit the media; billionaire **Vaughn Galloway** paid a **high ransom** for his freedom. The ordeal made Todd more precise, disciplined, and independent — and bound him to Galloway, in whose service (bodyguard → PMC) he would eventually command FIST.

**The Olympic kidnapping IS the Akrahuhum capture (LOCKED).** The brain-dump's Olympic-torch set piece is now canon as the *staging* of that capture: Todd, a decorated hero, is chosen to **light the Olympic torch**; terrorists posing as the Olympic Village construction crew have tunneled beneath it; in an orchestrated blackout on the eve of the opening ceremony the **Akrahuhum abduct him through the tunnels.** A captor's line: *"Welcome, Mr. Benchley. [the terror group] has been expecting you."* He is held and tortured for months; **Galloway, seeing the story break worldwide, pays the ransom** — exactly the locked beat — and Todd becomes his man. This keeps the locked "ex-Akrahuhum POW / Galloway paid the ransom / became his right hand" chain intact and gives the capture its cinematic origin. **The "wife Maria killed at the Olympics" beat is DROPPED** (see RESOLVED conflicts below): the wife **survives the capture and divorces Todd later.**

**Counterterrorism texture available to reuse** (brain-dump A1–A2, all `[PROPOSED]`): Todd's pattern-recognition at Olympic Park — construction anomalies, a heated worker/handler exchange, coded radio chatter ("Phase Two," "secure the VIP"), a chemical (explosive) odor — he connects the dots from his mercenary days but is ambushed in a low-traffic area, taken alive with stun guns + sedatives (capture, not kill) by overwhelming numbers. Good raw material for "The Man Forever Man."
