# ZEPHANIAH MWANGAZA — canon note  `[SOLID origin / PROPOSED power]`

**One-line:** Tanzanian soldier-seer and **Jawah Matu's mentor**; she opposed President Juma's war on Uganda, was betrayed, jailed, and decapitated — and her execution lit the coup.

> **Sourcing.** Creator originals `canon/characters/Jawah Matu .txt`, `canon/countries/COUNTRIES, SEASONS & AI.txt`. Swahili *mwangaza* = "light / radiance." Canonical spelling **Mwangaza** (original sometimes "Mwangaz").

## Identity
- **Zephaniah Mwangaza** — Tanzanian; female; loyal military superweapon turned quiet dissident against **President Ibrahim Juma.**
- **Mentor to Jawah Matu** (the albino sound-absorber).

## Power — `[PROPOSED — needs creator OK]`
The originals call her a "**time-traveler who can go into the future** + control minds." **Problem:** that collides with the **hard rule of exactly two time-travelers** (Asha → future; the China man → past). So her gift is being **re-cut to be adjacent but distinct** — she is **NOT a traveler:**

- **Foresight (precognition).** She **perceives branching possible futures** and can **project her mind forward to witness** events — but she **cannot physically travel**, and (unlike Asha) **cannot bring objects or people back.** She sees; she doesn't go.
- **Mind control.** She can override the will of those near her.
- **The combination is the threat:** she **knows what you're about to do, and makes you do something else.** That's why a regime would fear her enough to torture, then behead her — and why she could nearly topple a government.
- *(Alternative framings if you'd rather: "she only ever sees ONE fixed future and is cursed to watch it arrive," or "her foresight is unreliable/costs her sanity." Pick the flavor.)*

## Arc
Sees where Juma's war on Uganda leads → opposes it → **betrayed by a teammate**, jailed, tortured for information she won't give, **decapitated.** Her death **sparks the rebellion / coup** that Jawah Matu becomes a leader of. After her execution the regime refines the LSW drug **"Kuruka"** from her **cerebral fluid** (ties to the Jackals / LSW-fluid-harvesting thread).
