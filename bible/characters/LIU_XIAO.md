## Identity
- **Liu Xiao** — 60-year-old man, **Beijing**; **legally blind from birth**; an **outdoor / parlor massage therapist** working alongside other blind people in downtown Beijing.
- **Temperament:** stoic, self-sufficient, a quiet mentor figure. A smoker — `[canon]` the **Mother Muo's enhanced healing** is *why* he can keep smoking without consequence (insight-layer detail, not a vice gag).
- **Son — Lee:** picks Liu up from the parlor each evening. The night Lee fails to show is the night Liu walks home in the rain and is taken.
- **Sister (backstory):** years earlier the **CCP came for his sister** over the spheres; young Liu **hid** and was not caught. This is the wound under his wavering loyalty. `[PROPOSED]` she later resurfaces safe in the **United States** and urges him to defect; he refuses (conflict seed from the original).

## The event (origin)
Lee doesn't come; Liu sets out home in the pouring rain; hears an altercation; is **abducted by interdimensional/alien Muo travelers** (the same powers-granting force seeding LSWs worldwide). He wakes days later near his hometown in the mud, ringed by onlookers filming him (footage later scrubbed). Asked if he needs help, he says **"Yes"** — and the basketball-sized Muo spheres rise from the mud and attach to two bystanders, who gain temporary powers; one carries Liu into the air, and for the first time in his life he **sees the moon.** Neighbors call the authorities; the CCP arrives at the family home; Liu opens his eyes and can **see perfectly.** He walks out with the spheres arrayed behind him; the soldiers freeze. Scientists study him until they understand how the Muo "feed," and the President designates him **Protector of China.**

## Powers & bond
- **Mother Muo (cerebral implant):** an alien organ implanted into Liu's **cerebral cortex** that transmits his thoughts/feelings to the spheres and grants **enhanced healing** + night vision + heightened hearing. It is the *connection*, not a sphere.
- **Protector of China / national celebrity:** patriotized and weaponized as state propaganda. Per `bible/INBOX.md`, China **manufactures heroes** (a "People's World Hero?"-style state talent show) and Liu — blind masseur given sight — becomes one of the most beloved men in the country, hugely valuable to the CCP.
- **Wavering loyalty:** publicly the perfect patriot; privately marked by what the CCP did to his sister. This tension is the engine of his arc — and the thing **Zhang Wei** (the past-traveler) is sent to probe. See [[ZHANG_WEI]].

## Muo sphere mechanics `[canon]`
- **There are 3 of them. ✅ RULED 2026-06-26 (LOCKED): 3 Muo spheres.** (Earlier original fragments say 5; superseded.) Basketball-sized alien life-forms, psionically bonded to Liu and obedient to his will; he can summon them without speaking or gesturing.
- **Attachment:** a sphere attaches to the **back of the neck / spine**, tapping the neural pathways, to bond with a person.
- **Activation phrase — "Muo":** the spheres communicate by thought, but the user must first **say "Muo"** to open the channel and talk to the sphere.
- **The sphere controls power intensity:** it **mutes or amplifies** the granted power based on its own (dis)approval of the host — the sphere has a vote.
- **Tether / tentacle:** connected by a flexible tentacle reaching **up to 20 ft**, **typically ~8 ft**, able to shorten to a **minimum of 2 ft**. **Distance dictates strength** — the closer the host is to the sphere, the stronger the power.
- **Severance = off:** if the connection is severed, the **granted effects vanish** instantly.
- **Personalities:** each sphere has its **own distinct personality / temperament** — they are characters, not tools.
- **Temporary powers for others:** attaching grants a host a **temporary (often random) superpower**; detaching removes it.
- **Comfort range:** happiest within ~20 ft of Liu; pine to return if taken miles away for long; rest when their host sleeps; need no food or water.
- **Nanite phobia `[from original — verify if still canon]`:** Muo panic near **nanites** (e.g. Iron Eaters); prolonged exposure kills them, and a dead Muo emits a black "power" that seeds a **brain-eating nanovirus** turning the infected hostile and enraged. Possible tie to the Iron Eaters / Alliance-of-Four threat — flag for a creator ruling before using.
