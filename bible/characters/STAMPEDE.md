## Power — `[PROPOSED — upgrade from "gravity manipulation"]`
The creator wants it doper than generic gravity. Proposal: **John makes micro-singularities — collapse points** — and uses them two ways:

- **FLIGHT (black-hole surfing):** he doesn't go "up/down." He spawns a collapse point and *falls toward it*, spawns the next, and rides the gradient — **surfing his own gravity wells** in any direction. Visual: a man drifting **menacingly down a city avenue**, above the tanks and riot lines.
- **THE SWALLOW:** he reads a space first ("he looks around, he *knows* the space"), then opens a collapse point that **yanks everything toward it** — the *airplane-window decompression* image — and **compresses it to a tiny dense remnant.** People vanish into a marble. That's all that's left.

### The cost / limit (the no-plot-armor lever) — `[OPEN, needs creator]`
For him not to be an auto-win, the proposal gives him real rules:
- **He must "know the space" first** — a scout/lock requirement (a deliberate echo of the Xanthi's anchor). He can't collapse what he hasn't read.
- **Each collapse is physically punishing** — he can't spam Swallows; sustained use wrecks him.
- **Control slips when he loses it** — enraged, he's a danger to *everyone*, not just his target. (Matches "I want him to look menacing once he loses it.")

### Insight-layer note (the Crichton move)
Frame it as a **contained compression singularity**, not a true astrophysical black hole — a real one would evaporate (Hawking radiation) or never stop eating. His "wells" crush *locally* and wink out, which is *why* he can use them as tools instead of ending the planet. (Teleportation/gravity = solving a moving-coordinate problem; his gift is forcing a local one.)
