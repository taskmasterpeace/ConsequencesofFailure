## Who he is
A Nigerian (Igbo; hometown **Okigwe** per earlier ruling) **helicopter-company owner / pilot** — he **co-owned a helicopter company with a business partner** — grief-hollowed and lapsed-Catholic, who is remade into the **Memory Vessel.**

## The power — Life Form 0
- An alien **memory-slug** ("Life Form 0") that enters through his ear after a helicopter crash in Borno and **merges with his brain.**
- **What it is (creator-confirmed):** he holds **the memories of everyone who ever lived within a radius — a large portion of Africa.** Not just the dead in the abstract: he carries *everyone's* lives, loves, and knowledge across that span — and it **overwhelms** him. Because he holds everyone's memories, he **knows who has powers and everything about people** (e.g., he can know you through an aunt's memories of you).
- He can *hold* these memories, deliver them (e.g., a dead mother's forgiveness to her terrorist son), and **never be lied to.** Secondary gift: **empathy amplification** — people feel profoundly *heard* by him.
- **Cost (rent):** he is never alone; the multitude can drown him — the sheer breadth of memory is what overwhelms him; he struggles to find the borders of his own self/memory. (Earlier mechanics framing — cost scaling with how wide the vessel "opens" — is consistent with this: the wider he reaches across the radius, the heavier the rent.)

- **The real betrayal (creator-confirmed):** Kaiser co-owned the helicopter company with a **business partner**, and **the partner turned him in for being GAY** — Nigeria criminalizes homosexuality, so the denunciation is the genuine betrayal that breaks his life. This is the origin trigger.

## The dream (his arc)
**Born from being overwhelmed and from knowing everyone**, his core mission (creator-confirmed) distills to three promises: **a road to every home, water to every home, a laptop for every child.** Because he carries what everyone across the radius wanted, he knows what they all needed.

He builds this into a Pan-African movement to remake the **African Union**, driven by the things the dead all wanted — the **five promises**: **Digital Futures** (a laptop for every child), **All-Access Africa** (roads/water/power to every community), **Financial Freedom Framework** (independence from Europe, all 55 states), **Diaspora Unity Movement**, **Shield of Africa** (a real united defense). Climax (so far): addresses the **UN General Assembly** — *"a Memory Vessel… we are not seeking your permission."*

## Recruiting across Africa
Because the memories tell him **who has powers and everything about people**, Kaiser uses them to **recruit across Africa** — he already knows each person's grief, gifts, and history before he ever meets them. This is his method for assembling the movement.
