# KAISER EZIOBI  `[canon — from "The Memory Vessel"]`

**Definitive story:** `prose/kaiser/MEMORY_VESSEL.md` (the Expanded Edition). **This supersedes** the AI-drafted `prose/KAISER_AFRICAN_JOURNEY.md` and `prose/KAISER_ENHANCEMENT_SUMMARY.md` — treat those as obsolete (mine for nothing canon without checking here first).

## Who he is
A Lagos helicopter pilot (Igbo; hometown **Okigwe** per earlier ruling), grief-hollowed and lapsed-Catholic, who is remade into the **Memory Vessel.**

## The power — Life Form 0
- An alien **memory-slug** ("Life Form 0") that enters through his ear after a helicopter crash in Borno and **merges with his brain.**
- It carries **the memories, lives, and loves of the dead** — overwhelmingly **African** dead — and lets Kaiser *hold* them, deliver them (e.g., a dead mother's forgiveness to her terrorist son), and **never be lied to.** Secondary gift: **empathy amplification** — people feel profoundly *heard* by him.
- **Cost (rent):** he is never alone; the multitude can drown him; he struggles to find the borders of his own self/memory. He anchors himself on **Justus.**

## Origin
- Backstory wound: he **betrayed his business partner & best friend Justus** (J&K Helicopters) — refused him three days to flee — Justus went to prison and **died there.** This guilt is the spine of his character; it's the one grief Life Form 0 can't lift (Justus's memories aren't in the slug).
- Inciting event: flying a Korean couple to a **Borno** arms/cash deal with **Akrahuhum**; the deal turns to a massacre; his helicopter is RPG'd; the slug crawls into him in the wreckage. The terror leader **Faud** spares him because Kaiser delivers Faud's late mother **Miriam's** forgiveness.

## The dream (his arc)
Builds a Pan-African movement to remake the **African Union**, driven by the five things the dead all wanted — the **five promises**: **Digital Futures** (a laptop for every child), **All-Access Africa** (roads/water/power to every community), **Financial Freedom Framework** (independence from Europe, all 55 states), **Diaspora Unity Movement**, **Shield of Africa** (a real united defense). Climax (so far): addresses the **UN General Assembly** — *"a Memory Vessel… we are not seeking your permission."*

## Key relationships
- **Johnny Rain** (Uganda) — recruited via the grief of his drowned brother **Patrick**; the beloved public face of the movement.
- **Faud** — the reformed Akrahuhum leader; becomes Kaiser's devoted protector.
- **Aïssata** (Mali, reads groundwater) + the **boy from Mombasa** (forgets no face) — the gathered "strange and gifted."
- **Justus** (dead partner) — the anchor-grief.
