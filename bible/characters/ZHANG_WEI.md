- **Day role:** works at the **government massage facility for the blind** — the same state institution that employs **Liu Xiao**. This is where the two men meet and where Zhang's quiet surveillance of Liu begins.

## Power — travel to the PAST to audit loyalty
- He **travels into the past**, and his specific function is to **evaluate a person's loyalty to the Party** — reading the historical record of who someone *was* to judge who they *are*.
- **The Liu pattern:** going further and further back, Zhang Wei notices that **Liu Xiao seems *more* loyal the deeper into the past he looks** — i.e. Liu's loyalty is **strongest in the past and weakening toward the present.** That declining curve is the evidence that Liu is drifting from the Party now.
