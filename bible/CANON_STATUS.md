**What this is:** the answer to "what do we actually have?" Before we flesh anything out, this page tells you — per character and faction — *what real material exists, whether it's your original or the AI-rewrite layer, and where it contradicts itself.* Skim it, correct anything wrong, and this becomes the foundation everything else is built on. ## THE GOLDEN RULE
**Your originals + your word always beat the AI-rewrite layer.** When they conflict, the original wins (this is how we fixed Stampede). Nothing gets invented into canon silently — anything I propose to fill a gap is marked `[PROPOSED]` until you bless it.

## THE TWO LAYERS
A quick heuristic that holds up across the repo:
- **TRUST — your hand:** lowercase, natural-name files like `Stampede.txt`, `Eziobi.txt`, `Episode 0.txt`, `COF Notes.txt`, the Atlas Protocol notes, `Deck 52 Rachel Maddow_.txt`. This is the source of truth.
- **VERIFY — AI-derived:** ALL-CAPS `.md` files — `COF_MASTER_CHARACTER_DATABASE.md`, `BATCH_*`, `NOVEL_PART_*`, `CHARACTER_*.md`, `*_ENHANCED/_SUMMARY`, `THE_AFRICAN_UNION_CRISIS.md`, `COF_UNIVERSE_ANALYSIS.md`. Useful, but a prior automated pass reworded and sometimes *reinvented* things here. Verify against the originals before trusting.

## CONFIDENCE LEGEND
- ✅ **SOLID** — grounded in your original; trust it.
- ⚠️ **CHECK** — the AI layer diverges or embellishes; verify against your original.
- ❓ **THIN** — barely exists yet (a line or short sketch).
- 🃏 **SCATTERED** — rich material exists but spread across files and/or self-contradictory; needs consolidating + a ruling.
