# THE SAGES ("the knowledge people")  `[canon]`

## What a Sage is
A Sage wakes with the **complete, perfect knowledge of one specific college course / discipline** — the whole of a subject downloaded as if they'd maxed out a college major — **but with no practical experience** applying it. Brilliant and helpless at once: they *know* everything about their field and have never *done* any of it, which makes them utterly dependent on whoever has the resources, equipment, and people to point them at. (A genetics Sage still needs a lab; a medical-knowledge Sage still needs a hospital.)

## The late reveal `[important]`
**The world does NOT know that Sage knowledge comes from specific college courses until much later.** Early on, Sages are just inexplicable savants; the *source* of their knowledge is a mystery that gets uncovered well down the line. Treat "Sage knowledge = college courses" as a **drip / late reveal**, not something to state up front.

## Era
The Sages **rise around Year 2** ("The Sages" season/era) — this is the window **I Deserve Better** runs in (its TASK AI operating during the rise of the knowledge people).

- **Cissy Oliva** (Uganda) — **genetic-engineering Sage**; godlike knowledge of genetics, no experience; becomes Uganda's richest "prisoner" / the custom-animal empire. *(Spelling ruled: **Cissy**; old file says "Sissy Oliver.")*
