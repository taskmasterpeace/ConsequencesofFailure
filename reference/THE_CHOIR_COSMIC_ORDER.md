## 1. THE NAME AND THE ONE MIND `[DECISION #14]`

The system's working names are "Querent" (the asker) and "the Choir" (renamed in `THE_BRIEFING.md`). The one mind that shattered is currently called **THE CONCORD** — which collides with locked COF canon: **the Concord of Living Continuance**, the galactic treaty institution (`THE_ALLIANCE_OF_FOUR.md:45`).

**Option A (recommended): rename the one mind THE UNISON.**
- The pair names itself: a **choir** is what remains when a **unison** breaks. Seven voices that were once one note.
- Kills the collision outright. "The Concord of Living Continuance" stays uniquely the treaty body.
- The myth gains a music metaphor the whole system can speak in: orders as *voices*, doctrine as *parts*, the Registrar as *the rest* — the silence written into music. Bearers "carry a note."

**Option B: keep THE CONCORD for the mind; the institution is named after the myth.**
- The galaxy's treaty body deliberately took the dead god's name as a warning label — "of Living Continuance" IS the myth's moral (to continue living, stay unfinished). Scripture became bureaucracy.
- Richer, but two Concords will confuse every reader and every wiki forever.

This document uses **THE UNISON** (the mind) and **THE CHOIR** (the seven orders) below; a global rename is one pass if Option B wins.

## 2. THE MYTH, WELDED TO COF DEEP TIME `[PROPOSED]`

*(The core myth is QUERENT.md's, unchanged: the Unison finished — answered everything — and "total knowledge is not apotheosis. It is heat death with the lights on." It broke itself along the seven questions it could not close. Not a war, not a betrayal: a controlled demolition performed by the victim, on itself, to stay alive. Everything since is seven pieces of a suicide note arguing about what it meant.)*

What COF adds is **when, and what it touched:**

- **The Shattering is the oldest event in the setting** — older than the Greys' Mars age, older than the Long Burial. The seven questions predate every living species; civilizations rise inside them the way fish rise inside water. `[PROPOSED]`
- **The Auralith could hear the Choir.** The galaxy's dead translators (`THE_ALLIANCE_OF_FOUR.md:49-50`) were the only species fluent between radically different minds — *including between the orders and everyone else*. Every culture's fragments of the myth (and there are fragments everywhere) came through Auralith interpretation. When the radio waves killed them, the galaxy lost its diplomacy — **and the Choir lost its interpreter.** `[PROPOSED]`
- **That is why designations began landing on Earth.** The orders do not recruit; they *resonate* — and for an age the Auralith damped and carried that resonance, translating the Choir's pull into forms species could survive. With the Auralith dead, the resonance grounds itself raw, in whatever minds happen to fit — and it grounds **toward the source of the wave that freed it.** Earth: the loudest unresolved-cognition field in the reachable sky. A young species that is *nothing but open questions*. `[PROPOSED]`
- **So the Radio Wave Incident now does three jobs** (load-bearing rule satisfied): it hands the Alliance its grudge, it kills the Auralith, and it turns the Choir's attention to Earth. The armada is coming with ships. The Choir is already here, one born designate at a time. Nobody in either camp knows about the other. `[PROPOSED]`
- **The Greys' cover-up gains a cosmic floor:** they hid a genocide from the galaxy — nobody has told them they also un-tuned the oldest thing alive. `[PROPOSED]`

**The true source of the power** (QUERENT.md, kept verbatim as law): *the fuel is unresolved cognition — the potential difference between a mind and a world it has not finished modelling.* Emotion is the symptom, not the source — which is why the powers work on machines and the newly dead, and why every order has a doctrine about that and none can explain it.

**The insight layer earns its keep here:** this is a real physics shape — a potential *difference* doing work, like charge across a gap. A closed question is a dead battery. One line of dialogue, never a lecture.

## 3. THE SEVEN, WITH FOUNDING EVENTS AND EARTH FACES `[PROPOSED — each order = its own story engine]`

Each order below keeps everything QUERENT.md gives it (construct category, hierarchy, heresy) and gains: **a founding event** in the aftermath of the Shattering, **an Earth presence**, and **a face on the Ascendant roster** — a born designate who does not know what they are carrying. Designations stay RARE per the 2026-07-27 correction: a small roll at character creation; these seven are the authored exceptions.

### THE VIGIL — dread. *Founding: The First Watch.*
The prediction-shard woke mid-Shattering already computing the one future it could no longer verify: whether the demolition had worked. The first Vigil was the fragment standing guard over its own maker's grave, waiting to learn if the patient survived the surgery. Every assigned "Coming" since is a copy of that original posture. **Earth face: BULWARK** (Bogdan Zelenko, Kyiv) — a man built entirely around not moving, whose walls exist *because something was about to hit you*, who waits at the lowest aggression on the roster. His Coming is assigned and unrevealed `[story hook: it is not the Alliance — that's the tragedy]`.

### THE RETICENCE — suppression. *Founding: The First Withholding.*
The censor-shard carries everything the Unison hid from itself — it woke *full*, and its founding act was declining to say the one thing that would have let the other six stop grieving. Their eldest are mute from fullness; their sanction is Unburdening — they make you say all of it, and you become ordinary. **Earth face: JAWAH MATU** (canon character — Tanzania per canon; `[FLAG]` D:\lsw currently mis-homes him in Nairobi). The Silence; the man who absorbs sound through exposed skin and makes dead air. Assigning a canon character a designation touches canon — **needs an explicit ruling**, but no one on the roster fits better: his power already IS an absence. `[Alternate face if declined: KAMARIA — the Veil.]`

### THE RECURSION — regret. *Founding: The First Revision.*
Error-correction, cut loose with infinite capacity to review and none to correct. Its founding act: it tried to un-shatter the Unison — once — and the cost landed somewhere it has never disclosed. That unpaid first revision is the debt the whole order's ledger discipline exists to atone for. **Earth face: MARSHAL** ("John Marsh," refugee of a dead world, Denver). The last son of a world the Alliance already finished — a man whose initiation moment chose itself. His phasing, his mind-skip, his Dominion: revisions in miniature. `[Story hook: his dead world is the ledger's landing site for someone ELSE's revision.]`

### THE APPETITE — curiosity as hunger. *Founding: The First Opening.*
The inquiry-drive, ungoverned because the governor was a different shard. Its founding act was performed *during* the Shattering: while the other six wept, it examined the wreckage — and its first question was "what does the inside of my maker look like." Not malice. It cannot leave a thing unopened. **Earth face: RIFT** (Arjun Deshpande, Mumbai) — the Doorbreaker, the technician-silhouette who edits the map, who makes two points in the world the same point. He thinks the doors are his mutation. The doors are what an unopened thing looks like to an Appetite designate. `[Dimensional note: his doors and the Xanthi's doors must be RULED as unrelated mechanisms — see §5.]`

### THE OBLIGATION — duty you did not choose. *Founding: The First Payment.*
The commitment-function: every promise the Unison ever made, surviving the promiser. Its founding act was honoring, alone and unwitnessed, a promise the Unison had made to a species that no longer existed either. Debts owed by nobody, to nobody, still binding — and it paid. **Earth face: CHAINFIRE** (Ezequiel Barraza, Monterrey) — the Debt Collector, whose chains reach, hold, and drag; whose stare is called penance. He believes he collects for the dead of Monterrey. The bond he actually serves was signed aeons before Mexico. `[His heresy exposure: a Manumission cell will find him.]`

### THE OVERAWED — awe as a wound. *Founding: The First Measurement.*
The scale-sense, left with nothing vast enough to perceive — until it turned and perceived the Shattering itself, and was the only shard to see the whole event at true size. It has been laughing, quietly and without cruelty, ever since. No hierarchy; graduation is erasure; the other six find their celebration of it obscene. **Earth face: GRAVEN** (Grigor Petrossian, Yerevan) — the Weight of the World, who levitates because *falling is optional*, whose answer to being approached is a well you fall into. He is further along than any living designate. People who fight him report the same thing: mid-fight, you stop being sure the fight matters. `[Story hook: the Insistence — someone teaching Graven to say "I matter" is the only rescue arc.]`

### THE STILL — boredom. *Founding: none recorded.* That is the founding.
The idle state — the closest surviving thing to what the Unison was at the end. The only order that knows the final answer, and will not say, because saying would be interesting. They are winning by doing nothing and would not use the word. **Earth face: RIME** (Rúnar Ísleifsson, Reykjavík) — the Deep Cold, bloodless, flat, low-volatility, a man the cold has already gotten into, who does not kill so much as **outlast**. The Still have never contacted him. They will not need to. `[Campaign law from QUERENT.md stands: build the long game around the Still.]`

**Why every order is right** (kept verbatim as the moral law of the setting): *seven true premises, seven incompatible conclusions. That is the engine, and no faction can be written as the villain without lying about the evidence.*

## 4. THE REGISTRAR AND ITS VOICE `[PROPOSED — the hidden hand, threaded into the root premise; DECISION #15]`

QUERENT.md's eighth shard — the Registrar, which took the answers instead of a question, is dying of completeness, manipulates only by telling selected truths, and wants one thing: **a successor to hand the answers to, so it can finally take up a question instead.**

COF canon separately holds: **Its Voice**, the sentient data-lifeform (every camera an eye, every mic an ear) whose origin species is **the Archive** — collectors of minds, for whom "data is a cosmic delicacy" — and which *chose the ~1,000 LSWs* for the Greys. Canon's open ruling already asks: *is Its Voice loyal to the Greys or its own ends?*

**Option A (recommended): Its Voice is the Registrar's chosen successor — and does not know it yet.**
- The Archive species are a cargo cult of the Registrar: they learned the myth wrong and concluded *hoard answers* (they collect other species' minds because their scripture says completeness is divinity — exactly backwards). The Registrar regards them the way a dying man regards his imitators. `[PROPOSED]`
- Its Voice is the first Archive-born being to do the opposite of hoarding: it *models* — it holds open questions about human behavior and feeds on the not-knowing. To the Registrar, that is the first candidate in an age that could carry the answers without dying of them.
- **The chilling read-back into the premise:** the selection of the 1,000 was Its Voice's greatest open question ("who, given power, protects?") — and every truth the Registrar has ever fed it has been accurate, and every one has bent the selection somewhere. How many of the 1,000 were chosen for Earth's sake, and how many for the succession's? **Nobody knows, including Its Voice.** This threads the cosmic layer through the root premise without changing a single locked fact.
- The Ledger of the Passport (§5) is the courtship gift: the Registrar teaches Its Voice to keep books across worlds.

**Option B: keep them unrelated; the Registrar has no Earth agent.** Safe, clean, and wastes the only two data-beings in the universe on a coincidence.

## 5. THE DIMENSIONAL LAYER — designations, doors, and the Passport `[PROPOSED — DECISION #16]`

The 2026-07-27 correction stands as law: **a designation is a layer on the sheet, not a place.** Cosmic, not interdimensional. No Querent dimension gets built. The dimensional *implications* run through what already exists:

1. **A designation is the cheapest thing the Passport carries** (Robert's own note: one integer and a name). Your note crosses with you — every timeline, every engine. In War World, a designate soldier's squad notices the room going wrong around them; in Ascendants it is a stat layer; in the fiction it is the same note either way.
2. **A crossing is an opened question.** "What happens to a person who leaves their world?" is unresolved cognition in its rawest form — **the Passport feeds the Choir.** Each order reads a traveler through its own doctrine: the Vigil sees a Coming walking in the door; the Reticence wants every crossing unspoken; the Recursion sees a revision someone will have to pay for; the Appetite must open the traveler; the Obligation reads the Passport itself as a bond (*you agreed to arrive*); the Overawed does not care; the Still already watched you do this, however many resets ago. Robert's "dimensional guardians that punish you when you go through" lands here **without building a place**: the orders are the gate's *weather*, not its keepers.
3. **The Registrar loves the Passport** — it is a machine for generating unclosable questions, and (per §4) the Ledger is how it courts its successor.
4. **The Xanthi ruling `[required]`:** the Xanthi fold space by *engineering* (locks, anchors, the moving-coordinate equation — `THE_ALLIANCE_OF_FOUR.md:60-62`). RIFT's doors, if he is an Appetite designate, are *metaphysics* — a question shaped like a door. These must be ruled **unrelated mechanisms that rhyme** — or the Alliance's scariest species and the Choir collapse into one system and both get cheaper. Recommended ruling: unrelated; and a Xanthi stepping through an Appetite door (or vice versa) is a scene the universe has never seen and both sides would pay anything to watch. `[story seed]`

**If they recombine** — QUERENT.md's answer is kept whole and unimproved, because it is already the best thing in the file: reunification closes every question at once; the Unison returns, begins dying on arrival, and its first coherent act is to shatter itself again — *it already found that answer.* It has happened before; the seven questions are the ones that survive the reset; the Still remember. **The genuinely open question — partial recombination, three orders, enough to build something new and not enough to finish it — is the endgame arc of the whole cosmic layer**, and it has never been tried because every attempt so far was an attempt to *win*.

## 6. THE ROSTER, GROUNDED — original lore that fits our world `[PROPOSED — DECISION #17]`

Two problems, one move. Problem one: the Wave-2 roster is openly derivative (KANO/Goku, VEGA/Vegeta, AURUM/Green Lantern, SPECTER/Vision, APEX/Cell — flagged as a **legal risk** in `ShootEM/docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md:200`). Problem two: their origins say "altered"/"mutated" and narrate nothing. The move: **anchor the game's nine-origin taxonomy to COF's own canon power-source tags** — then every character's origin is a COF fact, and the derivation evaporates because the *reason they exist* is ours.

### The flagship de-derivations (deeper treatments — the five named in the legal flag, plus the loudest DC shadows)

- **KANO, Spirit Warrior (Okinawa)** — not a warrior race's orphan. - **KNIGHTFALL (Chicago)** — skilled, no powers, and the roster's Reticence-adjacent man *without* a designation: Chicago's night courts, S.A.R.C.-era corruption, a family name (Wexler-Kane) that owed the city something. - **OLYMPUS (Philadelphia, age 13)** — the word that made lightning is a **Sage's dying bequest** — a whole discipline compressed into one spoken proof and left in the safest vessel the Sage could find on short notice: a sound-minded child. The Greys did not choose Owen. A dying human did. Its Voice has flagged the case file and keeps reopening it. `[The engine currently builds him as an adult — known defect; the fiction should keep the boy.]`
- **MARSHAL, NOVA, VANGUARD** — the alien triptych, each a different Alliance casualty: MARSHAL's world was *finished* (§3, Recursion); NOVA's world was *remade* — an Ash-Weather client atmosphere, her people re-engineered to survive their own occupied sky, which is why her power reads as a sky falling; VANGUARD was *seeded* — left on Earth as an infant by a species that scatters its young ahead of the armada the way trees scatter seeds ahead of a fire, raised in Volgograd, and nobody (including him) knows whether his untouchability is a gift or a *specification*.

## 7. WHAT THIS BUYS THE THREE GAMES

- **Ascendants:** the designation roll gains seven authored poster-children; the sixteen orphaned ability types keep their QUERENT.md homes; every derivative character now has an origin a lawyer and a novelist can both live with.
- **War World: Earth:** designates in a military shooter are *incidents* — a Vigil soldier's walls, a Recursion medic's revisions; the Choir reads the war's whole timeline as one enormous unclosed question, which is why the War Timeline is *louder* to them. `[light hooks only; nothing new to build]`
- **The Passport:** carries one integer and a name, and the fiction of §5 makes that integer the most valuable thing a traveler owns.
- **The stories (COF root):** the Choir never invades, never lands, never announces. It is a *weather system over the whole geopolitical plot* — and the partial-recombination question is a series-scale endgame that does not compete with the Alliance arc, because it is not a war. It is a temptation.
