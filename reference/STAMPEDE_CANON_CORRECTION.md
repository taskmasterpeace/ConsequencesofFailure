# STAMPEDE — CANON CORRECTION

**Decision (creator, this session):** Stampede is **John Rivers** — the original `Stampede.txt` version, backed by `canon/README.md`. The later "Marcus Williams" rewrite is **rejected** and is being corrected everywhere it appears.

---

## CANONICAL STAMPEDE (use this)

- **Name:** John Rivers
- **Alias:** "The Stampede" (media); styles himself **"Justice"**
- **Who:** Former **LAPD officer** (African American), Los Angeles
- **Power:** **Gravitational field manipulation** — crushes, pins, throws, shields by warping local gravity. Gained via **Grey abduction**. He wins through control and insider knowledge, not super-strength.
- **The inciting murder:** His **sister, LAPD Detective Rebecca Carranza** (a different surname hid their relationship), is **killed by a car bomb** to bury her investigation.
- **The villains — the Shooter's Club:** a corrupt **LAPD anti-gang unit** running an illegal gun-trafficking ring out of the **Police Firearm Training & Evidence Facility**. Supervisor **Dave Wilkerson** skimmed **44 firearms**; they sold **Police Chief Doug Allen's son** a stolen gun and got him caught with it to **blackmail the chief** into protecting them. Rookie **Brian Kegan** uncovered it and was bought off (raise + transfer). Retired detective **Franz Escobar** is later sent to stop Stampede and starts to sympathize.
- **Arc:** Rivers turns in his badge and **executes the cover-up chain, then whole precincts**, station by station — LAPD first, announcing the **NYPD** is next. Becomes an international fugitive. **Kaiser Eziobi attempts to recruit him**, which draws U.S.-government heat. The city splits: terrorist or freedom fighter.

## REJECTED REWRITE ELEMENTS (delete on sight)

- Name "Marcus / Marc Williams"; alias "The Cop on Rampage"
- Powers: kinetic enhancement, dermal density, 95 mph, 15-ton lift, hardened skin
- **Cousin "Shanice Williams"** killed by cops **"Headly & Reynolds"**
- **"I DESERVE BETTER" notes** pinned on victims
- The "LSW blood/tissue trafficking" conspiracy as his origin (the real corruption is **gun** trafficking via the Shooter's Club)

## RESOLVED (creator decisions, this session)

1. **Kaiser's death:** The **U.S. / Western intelligence assassinates Kaiser** in Lagos for publicly defending Stampede. **Stampede does NOT kill Kaiser.** (`THE_AFRICAN_UNION_CRISIS`'s structure is canon; the `BATCH_1` / Chapter 6 "Stampede kills Kaiser in a 7-minute fight" version is wrong.)
2. **Stampede stays domestic:** John Rivers **never reaches Africa** — he remains a U.S. fugitive (LAPD → NYPD). The Kaiser link is only that Kaiser publicly defended/tried to recruit him, which got Kaiser killed; Rivers carries the guilt.
3. **Threat level:** kept at **4**.
