## THE FIVE "ARMADA" CREATURES (the pitch's invasion species)

### 1. Cathedral Giants — *living planetary engineers* → **ADOPTED as "The Mountain-Eaters"** (one of our Four)
- Homeworld: super-Earth, 3× gravity. Size: 250–600 ft. Limestone armor; forests grow on their backs; six pillar legs; **no head**; hundreds of tiny eyes in cracks; organs glow amber through translucent bone.
- Biology: **eat mountains**; gut bacteria convert stone → rare metals that become their skeleton. Whole ecosystems live on them.
- Mind: slow (one thought can take weeks); millions of micro-neurons distributed through the body → **destroying one section barely affects them.**
- Threat: **accidentally terraform planets by walking.** In-armada role: **living siege-fortresses / logistics / occupation platforms** (carry drone factories, barracks, cannons, shields — "sinking one is like sinking a fleet").

### 2. Glass Swarm — *interstellar energy-predators* → galaxy bank (parked)
- 2-inch glass-bodied insects; prism wings, needle legs, no mouth; nearly invisible; millions act as one.
- Biology: **absorb electrical activity** — kill power grids, stop brains. Individually dumb, collectively genius (smarter the bigger the swarm).
- Pitch role: **recon / "the eyes"** — arrive months early, map militaries, copy languages/frequencies, disable satellites, blind Earth before Day 1. *(Note: overlaps our Iron Eaters + the Xanthi's perception-casting; parked.)*

### 3. Maw Kings — *apex ambush hunters* → galaxy bank (parked)
- Homeworld: perpetual darkness. Eight limbs, white skin, **no eyes**; torso unfolds into a giant circular inward-toothed mouth.
- Hunt by **imitating caves** — prey walks in, it closes. "See" via gravity distortion (heartbeat, blood flow, muscle tension).
- Pitch role: shock troops / urban-tunnel warfare ("if the lights go out, run").

### 4. The Archive — *civilization collectors* → **ADOPTED as the ORIGIN SPECIES of "Its Voice"** (NOT one of the invading Four)
- Smooth black floating body; thousands of orbiting spheres, **each holding a dead civilization's complete memory.**
- Biology: reproduces by **absorbing minds**; never kills directly — **convinces civilizations to upload themselves.** Absorbed species become immortal but no longer alive. Can briefly **recreate any extinct species from memory.**
- Horror line: *"We saved you."* — after deleting your freedom. → This is now the deep nature of **Its Voice's people**; data is a cosmic delicacy (like energy). See `bible/factions/ITS_VOICE.md`.

### 5. Ashborn — *atmospheric/planetary parasites* → **ADOPTED as "The Ash-Weather"** (one of our Four)
- Made of black volcanic ash held together by **electromagnetic fields**; constantly falling apart and rebuilding. Homeworld (pitch): Vul-Taruun, volcanic moon.
- Biology: **cannot exist in vacuum — needs atmosphere.** Invades by **replacing a world's atmosphere with microscopic spores**; every breath converts living tissue into more Ashborn. Government = "the Pressure Choir" (decisions via wind/charge/ash). Remember via ash-layers ("Strata-Keepers").
- Weakness: **heavy rain** (water breaks their EM structure). In-armada role: **atmosphere prep** — black storms, poison visibility, kill aviation/solar, make cities unlivable → *and make Earth livable for the others* (our biological reason for the phased invasion).

## THE PITCH'S TRIGGER SPECIES (the dead energy race)
- **The Auralith** — energy-based civilization living in EM shells of gas giants/pulsars/magnetospheres; structured waveforms; "cities" = harmonic frequency-bands; memory = sung into space, maintained collectively.
- Killed by Earth's escalating radio/radar/satellite/nuclear EM pollution flooding an Auralith nursery field — poison to them, noise to us. → our **Silent Requiem.**

Killing them **severed the nervous system of interstellar diplomacy.** That's why the whole Concord shows up over one death. *(We keep the species **nameless** in-story for now; "Auralith" = the eventual reveal name. `[PROPOSED]`)*
