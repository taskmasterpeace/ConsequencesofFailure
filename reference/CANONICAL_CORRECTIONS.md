## CHARACTER CORRECTIONS

### TIME TRAVELERS - THE TWO TRAVELERS (CORRECTED)

**OLD ASSUMPTION (WRONG):**
- Liu Xiao was the second time traveler
- Unclear who traveled where

**CORRECTED VERSION:**
- **ONLY 2 Time Travelers exist (hard limit)**
- **Asha (India)** - Travels to the FUTURE
- **Unnamed Chinese Team Member** - Travels to the PAST
- **Liu Xiao is NOT a time traveler** - he can give people powers using Mule Spheres (his actual ability)

**Asha's Future Travel Rules:**
- Ghost-like state when in the future
- Invisible/tries to make people ignore her
- Can retrieve ANYTHING she touches
- Can retrieve anything she interacts with
- **Cost:** Being a "ghost" slowly eats at her sanity/wit
- Gradually drives her crazy over time - her sanity deteriorates
- Season 6: She arrives from the future with critical revelation

**Chinese Time Traveler:**
- Unnamed character (NEEDS NAME)
- Can travel into Liu Xiao's personal past specifically
- Observes Liu Xiao's history
- **KEY DISCOVERY:** "The further back he goes into the past, the more loyal Liu Xiao is to the government"
- This is a pattern he discovers
- Initially suspicious of Liu Xiao (dangerous power ability)
- Trust must be built over time
- "Some dope shit that he says" once they know each other

**Time Travel Limitations (Both):**
- Cannot permanently change the past/future
- If they interfere with events, they snap back to original timeline
- No paradoxes possible because changes don't stick
- Retrieving objects/information is allowed
- Interfering with events = forced return

### THE SAGES - KNOWLEDGE-BASED POWERS

**REVELATION TIMING:**
- Year 1: LSWs with superhuman abilities appearing everywhere
- **Year 2:** Discovery that different type of power exists

**WHAT SAGES ARE:**
- Knowledge-based LSWs
- Have complete mastery of ONE specific subject
- Everything about that one topic
- Total expertise in chosen field

**CREATION METHOD:**
- Same technique as Rusty Richards' implantation
- Grays insert comprehensive knowledge into minds
- Can target any subject matter
- Often given to people who will use it or struggle with using it

---

### LSW SELECTION CRITERIA - GRAYSS' CHOICES

**GLOBAL POPULATION:**
- ~1,000 LSWs globally (all races, nationalities)
- Selected by the Grays (through abduction)

**SELECTION PARAMETERS:**
- Selected for "sound mind" / psychological stability
- NOT selected for military skill
- NOT selected for physical ability
- Selected because they won't become villains (mostly)

**RESULTS:**
- Only ~2 "bad" LSWs so far (confirmed by creator)
- Most are fundamentally good people
- But good people ≠ soldiers
- Creates tension: "morally sound but tactically useless"

---

### MILITARY SERVICE ADVANTAGE - STRATEGIC INSIGHT

**THE CONCEPT:**
- Countries with mandatory military service have LSW advantage

**ISRAEL EXAMPLE:**
- Universal IDF service requirement
- When Grays choose Israelis, they're choosing trained soldiers
- Israeli LSWs are already combat-trained
- Know tactics, discipline, chain of command
- Israel punches above its weight militarily because of this

**USA/OTHER COUNTRIES:**
- No mandatory service
- LSWs are random: musicians, teachers, accountants, mechanics
- Godlike powers + zero combat training
- Massive training problem: "You can lift a tank but you don't know how to clear a room"

**COMPARISON:**
- Trained LSW countries: Israel, South Korea, Switzerland
- Mixed LSW countries: USA, UK, France
- Adds realism and strategic depth to conflict

### THE GRAYS - THE BIG DECEPTION

**WHAT GRAYS CLAIM:**
- Radio waves from Earth went into space
- Radio waves caused destruction to alien civilization
- Grays want to shut down Earth's signals to stop "polluting space"
- This is why they created LSWs (weaponize humans for defense)
- Trying to help humanity survive

**THE TRUTH:**
- **It was actually the Grays' fault!**
- Gray technology/gateway CAUSED the radio waves to come through space
- Their tech malfunction devastated things
- **An energy-based species got ANNIHILATED** because of Gray technology failure
- This caused the **United Nations of Aliens** to form
- Alliance of Four tasked to shut down Earth's signals
- **Grays are covering up their own catastrophic mistake!**

**ASHA'S REVELATION (SEASON 6):**
