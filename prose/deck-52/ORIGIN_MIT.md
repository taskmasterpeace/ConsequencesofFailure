## Chapter 1 — A Cosmic Coincidence

MIT freshmen. Vaughn Galloway (lanky, well-dressed, mischievous) meets Charles Sapphire (bespectacled, guarded) at a bulletin board. Vaughn's father = the famous actor who claimed to be abducted by aliens (ruined his career/reputation → seeds Vaughn's UFO obsession). Charles: "I know a thing or two about family secrets." Shared passion for technology + ambition. Observed from a distance by Professor Helena Krieg ("Two brilliant minds drawn together by the cosmos…"). Friendship to be tested by rivalry, love, loss, and a looming threat.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Birth of Taskmaster

Charles bursts into Vaughn's dorm with an idea: a platform for anonymous exchange of goods/services without traditional currency — "a system built on trust, powered by cutting-edge cryptography" (cryptocurrency transactions). They name it **Taskmaster** ("commands respect and efficiency"). Mentors: Professor Krieg and Professor Ackerman. They launch it; instant global success; investors/journalists/tech giants. Toast: "the unstoppable force of Galloway and Sapphire." Unease about power/responsibility; seeds of future rivalry planted.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Cheat Check Controversy

Vaughn proposes "Cheat Check" — hire actors to flirt with users' partners to test loyalty. Charles objects (violation of trust). Vaughn pushes it through; media firestorm; controversy. Charles confronts Vaughn ("We built Taskmaster to empower people, not to sow doubt"). The rift widens — moral/philosophical divide. Meanwhile, a young actress **Emily Raine** [draft variant of locked canon **Emily Rhaige**] takes the stage and captures Charles's heart, setting events in motion. Partnership fractured.
