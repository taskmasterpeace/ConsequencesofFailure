# CONSEQUENCES OF FAILURE
## Prequel · Teaser — "THE DEALER'S HAND"

> *Draft. The cold-open teaser for the pre-powers (Sapphire–Galloway) prequel. Canon honored: $1.5M ransom era, Pres. Williamson, Akrahuhum, Charles Sapphire, Naomi Pierce, Todd "Shogun" Benchley, Emily Rhaige; the burned-woman chemistry (deuterium oxide / off-world oxygen) from `Benchley Series.txt`. Invented specifics flagged `[PROPOSED]`. The AI "sting" at the end is the Dealer's-Edge concept — kept deliberately ambiguous; can be pulled if you'd rather hold that secret longer.*

## TEASER

FADE IN:

**EXT. PACIFIC OCEAN — USS CARL VINSON `[PROPOSED]` — PRE-DAWN**

Black water. A carrier the size of a city, asleep. SUPER: *Two hundred miles off Guam.*

**INT. CARL VINSON — AFT ELECTRICAL WORKSHOP — CONTINUOUS**

A CORPSMAN kneels over **PETTY OFFICER DANA REYES** `[PROPOSED]`, 26 — sitting upright against a junction box, hands folded in her lap, uniform pressed. Like a woman waiting for a bus.

He presses two fingers to her throat. Nothing. He tilts her head back to start compressions —

— and a thread of pale smoke rises from her open mouth and *hangs* in the still air. Doesn't disperse.

He stops.

**LT. CMDR. HOLLIS** `[PROPOSED]`, 50s, nineteen years of ordinary explanations, holds an X-ray to the light: a chest cavity cooked to carbon. The skin around it — unmarked.

> **HOLLIS:** There are no burns. Nothing touched her. (beat) She's been cremated from the inside, and her uniform isn't even singed.

He looks back at the smoke. It's still there. Still hanging.

**SMASH TO:**

**INT. CRYSTAL CITY — UNMARKED CONFERENCE ROOM — DAY**

No name on the door. **REAR ADM. OKAFOR** `[PROPOSED]`, pulled off leave and furious about it. Eleven people. Two presentations. On the screen: two very large photographs of Reyes.

**NAOMI PIERCE** (40s, Deck 52, precise, honest to a fault) lands her close:

> **PIERCE:** Containment, chain of custody, the two best forensic pathologists alive. (beat) What I *can't* tell you, Admiral, is what killed her. Anyone who tells you otherwise this morning is lying to win a contract.

Silence. It's the most honest thing said in that room. It costs her the contract.

Across the table — **TODD "SHOGUN" BENCHLEY**, 50s, black cowboy hat, does not sit. He slides a tablet down to Okafor.

> **BENCHLEY:** Sir. My people already know what killed your sailor. Give me ninety seconds to ruin your morning.

He walks Okafor through the chromatography. Slow. Practiced.

> **BENCHLEY (CONT'D):** The water in her lungs isn't water. It's *heavy* water — deuterium oxide — at a concentration that doesn't occur in a body, or an ocean, or anywhere a person could've swallowed it. And the oxygen we pulled out of the burn? Ran it four times. Two outside labs. (beat) The isotopes don't match anything on Earth. Don't match the solar wind. Don't match the catalog at all.
>
> **OKAFOR:** ...Meaning.
>
> **BENCHLEY:** Meaning it isn't from here. And I'd bet my pension it isn't from this *solar system*. Deck 52 will sell you a clean process and a year of meetings. I'm the only lab that's already seen this and lived. (beat) Your clock isn't mine, Admiral.

*(He's lying about exactly one thing — FIST has seen it once, not twice. Okafor doesn't know that. The photographs are very large.)*

Okafor signs.

**INT. DECK 52 — CHARLES SAPPHIRE'S OFFICE — NIGHT (NEW YORK)**

High above a city he could afford to buy. **CHARLES SAPPHIRE** listens to a phone at his ear. PIERCE (V.O.), telling him they lost it. He does not raise his voice. He never does, anymore.

> **CHARLES:** He gave them certainty. (beat) We were right, and he was certain. People always take the second one. (beat) You did exactly right, Naomi. Don't ever do different.

He sets the phone down. On the desk, the only photograph: a woman laughing in a red dress — **EMILY.**

He goes very still. We PUSH IN.

And — for the first time — we *hear it.* A CALM VOICE, barely above the room tone, meant for no one but him. Faint, at the edge of his POV: a flicker of text we can't quite read — vocal-stress flags, a percentage, a predicted move.

He has not been grieving in that stillness. He has been **listening.**

> **THE VOICE** `[PROPOSED — name TBD: "The House" / "Emily"]`: He took the contract to be *seen.* He will be on television within the week. (beat) You don't need the body, Charles. You need the man who has it.

Charles's eyes move — the first truly alive thing in the scene.

> **CHARLES:** (barely) ...Then deal me in.

He turns the photograph face-down. Gentle. Final.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD: CONSEQUENCES OF FAILURE**

Beneath it, small, a breath later:

*the hand had been dealt.*

**END OF TEASER.**
