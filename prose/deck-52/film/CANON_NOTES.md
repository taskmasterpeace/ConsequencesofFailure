## Coverage pass & revisions (2026-07-10)

The package was put through three independent professional reads (feature studio reader, prestige-TV development exec, skeptical showrunner). Initial verdicts: feature CONSIDER-as-franchise-keystone / PASS-as-standalone; series DEVELOP; strong writing, fixable structure. Their consensus blocking notes were then **addressed** — what changed:

**Feature:**
- **Inert threat → real, escalating threat.** Added a second, deliberate incident mid-Act-3 (nine dead at a Chilean sky-observatory), turning the danger from "one jar" into "something with intent that knows we're watching." The film now resolves *its own* dramatic question (two enemies unite against a live, escalating threat) instead of deferring everything — no longer a pure backdoor pilot.
- **Climax logic repaired.** The "I've had it for a year" contradiction now clearly attaches to the *leak* (a year old), not the days-old Reyes data; Charles's "engineered, not just off-world" read is now grounded (isotope *enrichment* ⇒ a refinery ⇒ a maker, reasoned from his own nanofabrication expertise), and Vaughn *contests* it before folding.
- **Money trace de-cheated.** Pierce no longer "traces the money to the tarmac" (impossible through hawala); she matches the *targets* to the *vendetta network* — a credible Diamonds feat — and buries her own analyst's parallel discovery *after* she makes it (ordering fixed).
- **Narrator gutted to a cold bookend.** Cut the interpretive/pre-chewing Anchor lines ("Buckle up," "We'll get to the aliens," "Hold on to that," "it's shuffling"); the close is now sparse and resonant, not a card pun.
- **Unfilmable novelistic asides removed** ("the movie will spend the rest of its runtime paying for this frame," "the sequel has a name," "the loneliest frame in the film," etc.).
- **Clanging lines fixed:** Krieg's "cosmos" groaner; Charles's "no floor / your ceiling" pun at the climax (now the raw wound — "Emily's the only reason you're standing").
- **RoboForge seed made coherent:** power and feedstock unified — the nanites must *metabolize* matter to build, so they need a self-replicating, metal-devouring fuel (→ the Iron Eaters, later). "METAL-EATER," not a vague "METAL."

**Pilot (de-duplicated from the feature + de-passivated the lead):**
- **No more verbatim overlap.** The Crystal City meeting is now a 10-second reflection-flash from Pierce's POV (not a replayed scene); the corridor is re-staged in an elevator with new dialogue; the Charles/Pierce phone call is replaced entirely by the decisive-move office scene. The two shared flashbacks are **swapped for new ones**: the MIT bulletin-board meet → a **dorm poker game** where young Charles first turns his AI on *people* (rhymes with the pilot's reveal); the diner meet-cute → an **Emily-agency** scene (she's formidable after a career blow, not a prize).
- **Charles is now active.** He makes one decisive, illegal, irreversible move on screen — staging an "accident" to steal FIST's tissue sample — so the lead is an engine, not an oracle who narrates his own correctness. The pilot ends on that move *landing* (the sample on Cho's bench; Deck 52 secretly holds what FIST thinks it owns) — the live-wire hook, not the telegraphed "Vaughn will come."
- **AI reveal reframed as dramatic irony** (audience knows he reads people; the company keeps misreading him for seasons) — removed the HUD-overlay that made him a walking superpower.
- **Benchley's off-record call motivated:** he cracks his own boss's safe, finds a dozen hidden incidents, and breaks his loyalty-code because the command he serves turned out to be dishonest — compulsion, not idle risk.
- **"Deal me in" rationed:** Emily now *owns* the phrase (the apartment scene, "ante's the dishes"); the feature keeps "Deal me in anyway" as the once-only saga payoff; the pilot ends on a different button (the stolen sample + the photo set upright).

**Bible:** the Charles entry now sells a *patient, decisive* lead (Logan-Roy-still-not-passive), not an inert one; the five fault lines are split into **renewable engines** (Wildcard Question, Machines vs. Men, the poker-hand container) vs. **finite arcs** (Secret War, Succession, FIST rivalry), foregrounding what actually renews a 5-season show; the AI note is corrected to dramatic-irony.

**Known next-pass item (logged, not yet done):** the feature's single highest-leverage structural note — fully **inverting the flashback/present ratio** (make the present-day alien-evidence crisis the spine and excavate the origin as motivated recall under pressure, Godfather-II style) — is only *partially* addressed here (via the second incident + a firmer present through-line). A full page-one restructure to that shape is the recommended next draft. Emily's fridging is *mitigated* (more agency in her scenes) but not *solved* structurally; worth a dedicated pass.

## Title / positioning

Ends on the reluctant alliance that the whole COF saga needs. Batman-Begins structural cousin: the origin IS the movie; the war is the promise.
- **Series:** *DECK 52* (TV) — the ongoing corporate-thriller engine (the Court, the Secret War, the Wildcard Question, the succession, the FIST rivalry) running *alongside and into* the 8-year Alliance countdown. See `../series/DECK_52_series_bible.md`.
