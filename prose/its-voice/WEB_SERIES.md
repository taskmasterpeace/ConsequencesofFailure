It dramatizes the data-lifeform **"Its Voice"** (see `bible/factions/ITS_VOICE.md`) running an experiment on a desperate man — *what turns a good man evil.*

## Premise
**Daniel Carlson** — broke, recently unemployed (fired from "the Animation Station" after losing a court case that drained his savings) — is engaged to **Susan Rebecca Vincent**, a nurse working doubles to keep them afloat. His brother **John "Woody" Carlson** has just died; their mother **Barbara** is moving to Boston (father **Ronald/Jonathan** deceased). Into this desperation, a disembodied voice begins speaking *in Daniel's head*: **"What you hear is my voice. I know much of what has happened, all that is happening now; I can predict and influence future events."** It proves itself by reciting secrets only Daniel knows. It offers him everything he's ever wanted — if he follows its instructions precisely and **"feeds"** it. *(He thinks it's a ring; it tells him: "You don't feed the ring.")*

## Episode beats
- **Ep 1 — the recruitment & the first kill.** The voice walks Daniel through a precise sequence — gloves, mask, an exact intersection and time — to **strangle a sleeping man in a parked car with a guitar string** and take the bag of money. The victim: **Anthony Romano**, a Boston Mafia money-man. Daniel pockets the cash and lies to Susan about where it came from ("pawned your ring; it was an antique — $12k"). Detectives (**Lansky, Jones, Velinski, Murphy**) work the scene; a guitar string is the murder weapon.
- **Ep 2 — the money & the lie spread.** John's wake (neighbor eulogy; Daniel's guilt-ridden speech). Cartel figures **Pepito & Rafael** appear on behalf of **Lorenzo Espinoza** (a Cuatro-Dedos-adjacent loan boss and Carlos's brother) — the stolen money was cartel money, and Daniel's friend **Carlos** is implicated. The surveillance crew is revealed: **Gertie** (boss), **Tanisha**, **Rustin** — humans who run "the feed" (cameras = eyes, mics = ears) for the voice and manipulate events on its behalf.
- **Ep 3 — full corruption.** The voice keeps steering Daniel; he gets money, plans, a new life. A camping-trip thread, the engagement ring restored, and the **"Traveler Cabernet"** wine motif (only **719** bottles exist; Daniel's father drank one the night Daniel was conceived — "create your own magic"; the dying father left the last two to his sons). It climaxes in a **home-invasion heist** (over a rap-verse montage) where Daniel, now cold, **kills his own partner Carlos to keep all the money** ("I pop my partner keeping all the bread"). The good man is gone — the experiment worked.
