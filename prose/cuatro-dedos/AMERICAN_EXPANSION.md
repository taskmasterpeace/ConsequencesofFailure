## Chapter 1 — Diego "El Maestro" Rivera; Officer Dan Torres

**Diego "El Maestro" Rivera** — cartel boss, former mathematics professor; tailored suit; inspects fentanyl/meth shipments with mathematician's rigor; demands perfection. Henchman **Miguel "El Lobo" Santos**. Call from **Rafa**: rival **Arturo Ramirez** moving on their turf (Eastside docks); Diego orders **Ricardo** to handle it (lethal).

— **Officer Daniel "Dan" Torres**, small-town Montana cop; patrols amid a drug scourge; **Mrs. Caldwell** worries about her grandson + the Cuatro Dedos Cartel reaching Montana. Dan's brother **Michael** died of drugs (alley flashback; vow to fight). Under-resourced (three squad cars for the county). Foot chase, arrest of a suspect ("Daniel Torres… you think this changes anything?").

## Chapter 2 — Lucia "La Ingeniera" Gomez; Montana expansion

**Lucia "La Ingeniera" Gomez** — Diego's strategist/engineer. They plan the Montana expansion: vast unmonitored land, thin law enforcement, isolation; target the **reservations** (overlooked, underprotected) via jobs/protection (recruitment + coercion); build a network of local dealers; back roads + four-wheel drives; safe houses (old farmhouses, abandoned mines). Diego warns failure won't be forgiven. Diego's estranged son **Alex** — fled the life, sought refuge in basketball/anonymity ("Forgive me, mijo"); the cartel is his "other child." "Montana will be ours."
