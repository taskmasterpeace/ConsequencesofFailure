## 1. CROSS-PART CONTRADICTIONS

### 1.1 — **The Iron Eaters' arrival year is internally contradictory.** *(HIGH — the single worst continuity break)*
Canon (`PREMISE.md`, `CANON_STATUS.md`, outline) locks the Iron Eaters as crashing **~Year 4**. Three Parts disagree on *when the dust actually fell*:

- **YEAR_4** (¶8): *"It had been falling for **eleven years** before anyone on Earth knew enough to fear it"* and the crash happens "this Part" (Year 4). Eleven years before Year 4 = pre-Year-0. Internally that means the satellite was already decaying before the story began — defensible, but see below.
- **YEAR_3** (final §): *"The Iron Eaters were still a **fleck of dust riding a dying satellite** somewhere overhead, **a year from the ground**."* This is correct relative to Y4. Good.
- **YEAR_7** (¶3): *"**Six years earlier** the dust had ridden a dead satellite down into the green of South America."* Year 7 minus 6 = **Year 1**, not Year 4. **This contradicts Year 4's own crash date by three years.**
- **YEAR_7** also says the rift plan "had been sitting in a locked drawer… for **two years**" and the blight has spread for "**three years**" — three years of blight from a Year-4 crash lands correctly at Year 7. So Year 7 is internally inconsistent with *itself* (3 years of blight vs. "six years earlier" the crash).

**Fix:** Standardize on the crash = **Year 4**. In YEAR_7 change "Six years earlier" → "**Three years earlier**" (matches "three years" of blight and the Y4 crash). Verify YEAR_4's "eleven years falling" is meant as *orbital decay time before the crash* (fine) and not "eleven years of blight" (would be wrong) — it reads correctly as decay time, but a refiner should confirm the phrasing can't be misread.

### 1.2 — **The crash location: Pará/Marabá (Y4) vs. "green of South America" (Y7) — consistent; but the *grey scar* geography needs one anchor.** *(LOW)*
YEAR_4 places the crash precisely **west of Marabá, Pará, Brazil**. YEAR_7 and YEAR_8 refer to "the grey scar across the heart of South America" and "where the first satellite had fallen." These are compatible (Pará *is* the heart of South America's forest), but YEAR_7's rift opens "at the grey scar where it had all begun." Keep — just ensure no Part later names a *different* country for the crash. (None does. Good.)

### 1.3 — **The monster-virus origin year + contagion mechanics: small but real drift.** *(MEDIUM)*
- **YEAR_4** births the gene-body virus in Year 4 ("that same year"), contagious **only between LSWs** at first. Correct per outline.
- **YEAR_5** says it "had been born **the year before**" (Year 4) — consistent. Good.
- **YEAR_5** then gives the jump-to-ordinary-people mechanism: a variant arising in **trafficked, pooled LSW plasma** in the Jackal fluid-economy. This is a clean, well-built bridge — but it is **invented in Y5 and never seeded in Y4**. Y4 ends with the virus sealed behind "good seals" that "would hold for a while." The handoff works, but Y4 should plant the fluid-economy/trafficking vector as the leak risk so Y5's payoff doesn't feel introduced-at-need.
- **Naming:** Y4 calls it "the gene-body experiment / the monster-virus"; Y5 calls it "the monster-virus"; Y8 calls it "the gene-body experiment that birthed a virus." Consistent enough. No fix needed beyond keeping the term stable.

### 1.4 — **Liu Xiao's age: 60 vs. the "five decades as a masseur" math.** *(LOW — canon-adjacent)*
Canon: **age 60** (ruled). YEAR_2 says "sixty years old… four decades" reading the world through his hands. YEAR_6 says "sixty years old… **five decades** as a parlor masseur." Forty vs. fifty years of practice for the same 60-year-old man. **Pick one** (four decades is more plausible for a man who presumably didn't start at age 10). Fix in YEAR_6.

### 1.5 — **Zhang Wei's age & the loyalty-curve scene is told twice, nearly verbatim.** *(MEDIUM — see also §5 Duplication)*
Both YEAR_2 (¶"Which is where the other man comes in") and YEAR_6 (¶54–57) give: Zhang Wei = 42, a father, works at the same parlor for the blind, finds the "loyalty curve" (more loyal in the past, thinning toward present), sits on the verdict to buy his daughter a talent-show seat. Ages agree (good), but Year 2 and Year 6 narrate the *same discovery* as if fresh both times. Year 6 should treat it as **established and advancing** (he's still sitting on it; the Moon plan now races his verdict), not re-discover it. See §5.1.

### 1.6 — **The "Tragedy at Andrews" location wobble.** *(LOW)*
YEAR_1 says Cuba sent LSWs "to an Air Force installation **near Miami**" — but the event is named for **Andrews** (Joint Base Andrews is in Maryland, near DC, not Miami). The outline calls it "the **Tragedy at Andrews** (Cuba's LSWs in Miami)." So the *name* and the *place* are split in canon itself. **Flag for creator ruling:** is it "Andrews" (the base) used loosely, or should the incident be renamed for its Miami location (e.g., "the Tragedy at Homestead," Homestead ARB being the actual Miami-area Air Force base)? Currently YEAR_1 inherits the canon mismatch verbatim. Recommend renaming to a Miami-area base **or** relocating to Andrews; right now a knowledgeable reader trips.

### 1.7 — **Marletta's death: "something went wrong" (Y3) vs. firm statements elsewhere.** *(LOW — intentional open beat, but watch consistency)*
YEAR_3 leaves the botched extraction `[climax to design — creator]` and says Marletta "died… with the unborn child still inside her." YEAR_7 and YEAR_8 both treat her as dead by Sandra's hand — consistent. Good. Keep the open mechanism flagged, but the *outcome* (mother and child both dead) must stay fixed; no Part should later imply the child was taken. (None does.)

### 1.8 — **Stefanos's shapeshifting (Marletta's face): set up in Y3, **never paid off** in Y7/Y8.** *(MEDIUM — see §5.2 dropped payoff)*
YEAR_3 plants it hard: he "would use it, in the end, to wear his dead wife's face." YEAR_7 mentions he carries "the face of a dead wife he could put on like a coat" but does nothing with it. YEAR_8 drops it entirely. The single most haunting image set up for Stefanos has no scene. Either pay it off or down-grade the Y3 promise. (Recommend a short Y7 or Y8 beat.)

### 1.9 — **Faud's fate after Year 5.** *(LOW)*
YEAR_5 makes Faud the carrier of the Memory Vessel to the UN. YEAR_8 says "somewhere in the new order there was someone who carried what he had carried" — deliberately vague, doesn't name Faud. Acceptable (flag-don't-resolve), but a one-line nod in Y6 or Y8 confirming Faud (or a named successor) still bears the slug would close the loop the reader is holding.

### 1.10 — **Spelling/term audit (all consistent — recording for the loop):** "Akrahuhum" (Y2, Y4 ✓), "Its Voice" capitalization (✓ all Parts), "Living Super Weapon(s)/LSW" (✓), "Cissy Oliva" (Y2, Y4, Y5, Y6 ✓ — never "Sissy"), "Stampede/John Rivers/Rebecca Carranza" (Y3, Y5, Y8 ✓ — never Marcus/Shanice), "Benchley/Shogun" (Y1, Y2 ✓ — never Ravage), "Marletta" (✓), "Kuruka" (Y2, Y3 ✓), "Icchatām Ganga" (Y2, Y5 ✓). **The contamination flagged in CANON_STATUS does not appear in the novel drafts. ## 2. RECURRING-CHARACTER & THREAD CONTINUITY

### 2.1 — **Asha (India, future-traveler) — under-carried.** *(MEDIUM)*
Introduced richly in YEAR_2 (mongooses, memory-cost, near-future glimpses she can't hold). Then **absent from Y3–Y7**, and in YEAR_8 she reappears with a *new* framing: "the strategists of the Alliance had no answer prepared for an enemy who could remember a battle that had not happened yet." That's a great beat — but her power is *future-travel that costs memory/sanity*, not battlefield precognition, and her Y2 tragedy was that she **can't retain** what she sees. Y8 needs a re-anchor line acknowledging the cost (e.g., she pays in self to bring back even fragments) or it reads as a different, conveniently-buffed power. Also: the outline never gives Asha a mid-book arc; consider one seeded mention in Y5 (the Dying) since a future-seer in a plague is dramatically loaded.

### 2.3 — **Jawah Matu — set up Y3, dormant Y4–Y7, big payoff Y8.** *(MEDIUM)*

## 3. THE SEAMS (Year N → N+1 handoffs)

Each Part should end pulling toward the next and each should open re-grounding the reader in the year's "weather." Assessment + a proposed connective line where weak.

- **Y1 → Y2:** **Strong.** Y1 ends on the world choosing to fight apart; Y2 opens "the year after the impossible becomes ordinary… a thing stops being a miracle and starts being an asset." Clean thematic baton. *No fix.*

- **Y2 → Y3:** **Strong.** Y2 ends "the price of everything"; Y3 opens "the world had finished being amazed… a weapon looks for something to be pointed at." *No fix.*

- **Y3 → Y4:** **WEAK / abrupt.** Y3 ends grounded in human-on-human tragedy ("the sky was still empty"). Y4 opens cold on the satellite with no bridge from the human floor — the reader is dropped into orbit. Y3 *does* plant the satellite ("a fleck of dust riding a dying satellite… a year from the ground"), which helps. **Proposed connective beat** (open of Y4): a single sentence tying the empty sky of Y3 to the falling thing — e.g., *"The sky that Year Three had called empty was not empty. It had been carrying something down for a long time, and in the fourth year it finished the fall."*

- **Y4 → Y5:** **Strong.** Y4 ends "the doubling had begun… you know already what the fifth year is named." Y5 opens "There is a number the living were never meant to know." Excellent. *No fix.*

- **Y5 → Y6:** **Strong.** Y5 ends with survivors beginning to imagine a new order, "the name would come the next year, out of the ash." Y6 opens "out of the ash… the impulse to make an order." Near-perfect handoff (almost too literal an echo — fine). *No fix.*

- **Y6 → Y7:** **MEDIUM.** Y6 ends "The clock kept subtracting. Two years left." Y7 opens "By the seventh year the war had no front." The *blight* is the throughline but Y6 barely advances the Iron-Eater war (it's offstage in Y6, which is the New-Order/Moon Part), so Y7's all-out-blight-war opening feels like a gear-jump. **Proposed:** Y7's opening could nod that the leash-building of Y6 happened *while* the blight kept spreading unchecked — one clause reconnecting the two. (Y7 ¶3 mostly does this already; verify it lands.)

- **Y7 → Y8:** **Strong.** Y7 ends "the sky was already beginning to darken… the eighth year was coming." Y8 opens "The shadow came without a sound." Ideal. *No fix.*

**Seam summary:** 6 of 7 seams are good-to-excellent. Only **Y3→Y4** genuinely needs a bridge; **Y6→Y7** wants a clause.

## 4. VOICE / STYLE DRIFT

House style (locked): third-person **omniscient storyteller**, **narrative summary**, **free-indirect discourse**, **NO quotation marks / minimal direct quotes**, built to be read aloud.

- **Quotation marks:** **Clean across all eight Parts.** I found no dialogue in quotation marks anywhere. Speech is rendered indirectly throughout. This is the single most consistently held rule. ✓

- **YEAR_3** uses an **italicized direct-quote device**: *"now he has a gun, as you can see"* (the planted-gun line), *"my son has to go home,"* *"Tyrell deserved better. I deserve better."* These are quotes without quotation marks (italics instead). It's a gray area — technically inside the "minimal direct quotes" allowance and arguably free-indirect, but Y3 leans on it more than any other Part. **Minor drift; acceptable, but flag for consistency** — decide whether italic-quotes are a sanctioned device book-wide (Y5 uses it too: *"they're frightened" / "good"*; *"Life Form 0 is not inside"*) or should be folded fully into indirect speech.

- **"The listener" vs. "the reader":** Parts address the audience differently. **Y4 and Y5 repeatedly say "the listener"** ("the listener should understand," "the listener handles steel"). **Y1, Y3** say "a reader." **Y7, Y8** use "the storyteller will not pretend…" Both fit "built to be read aloud," but the second person varies. **Recommend standardizing** on one (the audio focus argues for "the listener," but "the storyteller"-as-narrator is the most elegant and is used in Y4/Y5/Y7/Y8). *Low priority, but a quick global harmonization improves unity.*

- **"The storyteller" as self-referential narrator:** Heavily used in **Y4, Y5, Y7, Y8** ("the storyteller will not pretend," "the storyteller asks you to hold onto"). **Absent in Y1, Y2, Y3.** This is the clearest *voice* split: the back half of the book has a more visible, self-announcing narrator than the front half. Not wrong — but a reader feels the narrator "wake up" at Y4. **Recommend:** either thread one or two light "storyteller" gestures into Y1–Y3, or trim the most self-conscious ones in Y7–Y8, so the narratorial voice is even.

- **Insight layer:** Excellently varied and well-distributed — radio-wave wake (Y1), Muo tether/NuroNuro voltages (Y2), sound-as-pressure & the spiked motes (Y3), orbital decay & self-replication math (Y4), virus-as-copying-machine (Y5), life-support/gel & time-travel-as-reading (Y6), "it's not about distance"/rift-as-frequency (Y7, Y8). **No two Parts repeat the same *kind* of insight back-to-back.** This is a model execution of the rule. ✓

- **Tone:** Consistent grounded-geopolitical-thriller throughout. No Part goes capes-and-tights. Y4 is the most "hard-SF," Y5 the most elegiac, Y8 the most apocalyptic-grand — but all stay in register. ✓

**Voice summary:** No Part *breaks* the rules. The drift is the **narrator's visibility** (front half quiet, back half loud) and **"reader/listener/storyteller"** inconsistency. Both are easy harmonizations.

Y8 *does* deliver the Xanthi door-walking but **never references the existing scar** as the door they exploit. The Y7 setup ("a door already half-broken") is dropped in Y8. **Fix:** one Y8 line tying the first/worst breaches to the Year-7 rift-scar would pay off the seeded promise and tighten the Y7→Y8 spine.

### 5.8 — **The Memory Vessel's "cost scales with how wide you open it."** Y8 introduces this mechanic ("the cost of opening it scaled with how wide you opened it") as if established, but it's **new in Y8** — Y5 (the Vessel's big Part) never states this scaling cost. It's consistent with canon (`COF Notes` / Memory Vessel notes), but inside the novel it appears without setup. **Fix:** plant the scaling-cost in Y5 where the Vessel is central.

## 7. VERDICT
