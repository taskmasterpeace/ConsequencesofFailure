# CONSEQUENCES OF FAILURE — THE NOVEL (first draft)
## Master year-by-year outline — the writers'-room spine

> **Format:** 8 documents, one per Year (Y1→Y8), the 8-year countdown to the alien arrival. Each is a long novel "Part" (~5,000 words → ~4.5 hrs total audio).
> **Style (non-negotiable):** third-person **omniscient storyteller**, **narrative summary**, speech in **indirect / free-indirect discourse** — *no quotation marks, minimal-to-no direct quotes.* Longer, novelistic, built to be read aloud. The reader is *carried* through a grand scope and *sees* how it all connects.
> **Best practices:** open each Part on a strong, concrete image; when a character returns, re-anchor them in a line so a listener remembers; stage the moments characters *meet* with weight; weave the "insight layer" (Crichton/Star Trek — explain a phenomenon plainly, varied, never a lecture); make each **country feel like that country** (lean on `canon/countries/Country Master Sheet - Country.csv` + real places/language/issues). Sources of truth: everything in `bible/`.

## YEAR 1 — "THE WARNING"
**Open on the cosmos, not on Earth.** The Greys — dimensional travelers who jump between here and "there" — throw off **radio waves** as they move, and those waves are lethal to a fragile **energy-based species**, which they wipe out without ever meaning to. *(Insight layer: radio waves crawling across space for ages; why a moving thing leaves a wake.)* At an **intergalactic council**, the question of blame; because radio waves are slow and **Earth is a loud, broadcasting world**, the death is pinned on humanity — whether by genuine error or because the guilty Greys let it land on us. The aliens **rename Earth.** Four militarized civilizations resolve to come. The Greys, guilty and "over this part of Earth," choose to prepare us instead — and the way they prepare us is the engine of everything.
**Earth threads:**
- **Zuma Rock, Nigeria** — a Grey site; the place the otherworldly first touches the ground. (Ground the origin in a real, specific place.)
- **Rusty Richards** taken from his yacht (the awards-night party where human artists beat the AI music for maybe the last time; his friend **2 Glocks**). Two years gone.
- The Greys' method: they don't *give* weapons, they **turn people into weapons** — and to pick the right ~1,000, they outsource human **psychology** to **"Its Voice,"** the sentient data-lifeform (every camera its eyes, every mic its ears). *(Why no supervillains: each is chosen for who will protect, not prey.)*
- **Rusty returns**, the press conference, the warning; people worldwide reappear changed (LSWs).
- The scramble: nations enlist their LSWs; the **Tragedy at Andrews** (Cuba's LSWs in Miami); the **Living Super Weapon Threshold Treaty** (governments can't wield LSWs — only companies); **Vaughn Galloway → FIST**; **SPEAR proposed at the UN and rejected.**
**Feel:** USA, Nigeria, the UN floor. **POV anchors:** Rusty (herald/"Tomorrow Man," non-powered); a Grey/Its-Voice cosmic narrator thread.

## YEAR 2 — "THE GIFTED WORLD"
The world learns what it's holding. Country by country, the LSWs become instruments of power.
- **China:** Liu Xiao (blind Beijing masseur → sighted via the **Muo spheres**), the *People's World Hero?* game show → the **People's World Army**; **Zhang Wei**, the past-traveler quietly auditing loyalty, beginning to watch Liu.
- **Nigeria:** **Kaiser Eziobi** — S.A.R.C. brutality, his exile; the helicopter, the massacre, **Life Form 0**, the memories of the dead (the Memory Vessel begins).
- **India:** **Establishment 24** rebuilds the nation (the **Icchatām Ganga**), Colonel **Reddy** pressured to militarize; **Asha**, the future-traveler.
- **Armenia:** **Karine Abrahamian** / NuroNuro brain-sharing.
- **The Sages** ("the knowledge people") begin to surface — complete knowledge of one college course (the *source* a late reveal); **Cissy Oliva** (Uganda).
- **The Jackals** and the LSW-fluid economy take shape ("not just blood").
- **Deck 52 vs FIST:** Charles Sapphire and Vaughn Galloway — the corporate-shadow war the LSW era inherits; **Benchley** at Galloway's side.
- **"Its Voice"** runs its experiments on ordinary people (Daniel Carlson among them); its true aim bends toward **Sandra** (→ DC).
**Feel:** China, Nigeria, India, Armenia, Uganda. **POV anchors:** Kaiser, Liu, Reddy.

## YEAR 3 — "BLOOD AND PRECEDENT"
The gifts turn into weapons against each other; the first monsters are human.
- **Greece/Turkey:** **King Stefanos (Alexandr)** — first celebrity superweapon; the Jackal **Sandra** comes for his pregnant wife **Marletta**; the break-in; the **4 days** of devastation on Turkey; he becomes the **first LSW branded a villain/fugitive.** *(Insight: his power — energy that *forms* then *fires* or *propels*; the jagged spikes that make precision impossible at first.)*
- **Tanzania:** **Jawah Matu** (albino sound-absorber) and his mentor **Zephaniah Mwangaza** (the seer); President Juma's war on Uganda; the drug **Kuruka**; the coup.
- **USA, grounded floor:** the **Shooter's Club** (Bonny, *Condemned*); **Stampede / John Rivers** rising from his sister **Rebecca Carranza's** murder; the **"I Deserve Better"** killing of **Tyrell Lober** (Headly shoots & later suicides; Oliver covers up) → the movement.
- **Mexico:** **Jance Bloomberg** and the **Cuatro Dedos** cartel (Diego "El Maestro" Rivera dies → Jance takes the dead heir's face); the **Clown-Sheriff (Ramiro Guzman).**
**Feel:** Greece, Turkey, Tanzania, USA (LA/Union City), Mexico. **POV anchors:** Stefanos, Jawah, Stampede, Janine Lober.

## YEAR 4 — "THE IRON EATERS"
**Open on the Iron Eaters' arrival — the satellite.** A fleck of alien **"dust"** rides in on a derelict **satellite**; the satellite's orbit decays and it **crashes in South America**, seeding the **Iron Eaters** — self-replicating nanites that *consume metal.* *(Insight layer: how nanites eat metal for energy/material; why "dust" is the right word; why infrastructure is the first casualty.)* They are the **precursor / "softener" wave** — not the main armada, the scouts any invasion sends ahead.
- **Deck 52** captures and weaponizes them: **RoboForge v2** (Charles weaponizing the plague eating the world).
- The world's metal sickens — power grids, machines, weapons; panic.
- **The gene-body experiment:** a genetic experiment involving two superpowered people goes wrong → a **virus that turns people into monsters** (first only LSWs).
**Feel:** South America (the crash), USA (Deck 52). **POV anchors:** Charles Sapphire, a South-American first-witness, Benchley.

## YEAR 5 — "THE DYING"
The virus mutates and reaches ordinary people; the world comes apart.
- Pandemonium; nations fall; the death toll climbs toward **half the world.**
- **The Sages find a cure** — but too late for most. (Their finest, costliest hour.)
- Kaiser's Pan-African dream crests — and the **U.S./Western powers assassinate him** for defending Stampede; the **Memory Vessel** at the UN.
- Amid collapse, the survivors begin to imagine a new order.
**Feel:** global; Africa (Kaiser); the hardest-hit regions. **POV anchors:** a Sage, Kaiser (his end), a survivor-everyman.

## YEAR 6 — "THE NEW WORLD ORDER"
Out of the ash, the **United Nations of the New World (UNNW)** forms — rebuild, re-law, re-order; LSWs heavily regulated. But the old hungers return; factions jockey; the Iron-Eater blight is still spreading.
- **Moses Apio / Uganda** (the Atlas Protocol symbiont) in the rebuild.
- **China's resistance** begins to dream of the **Moon escape** — fleeing the CCP to start a new society, enabled by the growth-gel + certain LSW powers. *(Insight: why the Moon, what it costs.)*
- Deck 52, FIST, Establishment 24, the People's World Army — the powers re-arm under new names.
**Feel:** Uganda, China, the new global capital. **POV anchors:** Moses, a Chinese-resistance figure, a UNNW founder.

## YEAR 7 — "THE LAST STAND"
The Iron-Eater war goes all-in; humanity throws every LSW at it.
- A massive **joint LSW assault**; the Iron Eaters adapt and counter; heavy losses on both sides.
- **Rusty Richards** rallies the leaders (he doesn't fight — he *unites*); a desperate plan forms: tear a hole and **banish the Iron Eaters to another dimension.** Risky, costly — and it *works* (they're gone), but at a price.
- The **Moon escape** executes against the chaos.
- Threads converge; old enemies forced together (Stefanos & Sandra).
**Feel:** worldwide war; the dimensional rift. **POV anchors:** Rusty, the LSW strike-team, Stefanos/Sandra.

## YEAR 8 — "CONSEQUENCES OF FAILURE"
**The sky goes dark: the Alliance of Four arrives.** The Slaught, the Deep, the Strategist Race, and the **Xanthi** (the Doorwalkers — bodies through dimensional doors, the "anchor/mark" that makes the marked a permanent breach). Everything the Greys feared, and everything humanity squandered by fighting itself, comes due. *(Open on the shadow crossing the Earth.)*
- The cost of the 8 years of disunity; who's left; who rises.
- The Greys' gamble — did turning us into weapons, one careful soul at a time, give Earth a fighting chance? End on the threshold of the war the title always promised.
**Feel:** the whole world at once. **POV anchors:** the surviving ensemble; Its Voice (it has watched all of it).
