# I DESERVE BETTER — "The Tragic Story of Tyrell Lober"  `[PRODUCED short — live action, ~8:51, Machine Kings Media]`

> One of the already-produced catalog pieces (`reference/PRODUCTION_INVENTORY.md`). Clean capture from the film transcript; raw available on request. This is the **origin tragedy** of the "I Deserve Better" movement.

## The story
- A **911 call** reports an intruder at a house. Police respond and **shoot and kill Tyrell Lober**, a young Black man, claiming he had a gun / pointed a weapon — *"you were in fear for your life and the safety of your partner."*
- **The "weapon" was a game/drone controller.** Tyrell was there over a **drone** he was buying from an online seller (who'd held it for him). *"It's a goddamn game controller — we killed this kid over a toy."* Then the cover-up: *"now he has a gun, as you can see."* IA and the department rule it open-and-shut; the officer goes on administrative leave.
- **Janine Lober** — Tyrell's **sister**, a **museum curator at the Union City Art Center** — refuses to let them brand him a thug (*"a D in math"* but *"an A in computer technology"*) and leads the protests. The rallying cry becomes **"Tyrell deserved better — I deserve better — we all deserve better."**
- **Det. Deborah Winston** — **Internal Affairs** (Uniontown/Union City PD) — keeps digging despite pressure, gets the drone seller's account and the controller-not-gun proof, and takes it to the media (**Sheldon Lewis**, producer at WUTX; reporter **Dana Barnes**, WMKSU) — *"quite possibly the biggest cover-up in Uniontown history."*
- **Outcome:** the truth comes out. **Officer Headly** fired the fatal shot — and **later commits suicide** (the thread's heaviest beat); **Officer Timothy Oliver** ran the cover-up and is held for it. At least a measure of justice.
- Closes on a song — the movement's anthem (*"I deserve better… begging somebody for a job, unfair wages, fighting for twelve dollars"*) — the social-justice spine of the whole thread.
