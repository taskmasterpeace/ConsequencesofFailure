**Rusty Richards returns from alien abduction with warning.** Alliance of Four will arrive in 8 years. Global panic begins. First LSWs (Living Super Weapons) emerge worldwide. Governments begin assembling their own powered individuals.

### Timing
- **1.01 (January):** Rusty Richards mysteriously returns from space, announces 8-year countdown
- **1.02 (February):** Major events cascade globally
- **1.02-1.08 (February-August):** Raghavan Reddy in Himalayan isolation
- **1.08 (August):** Kedarnath rescue/recruitment
- **1.09+ (September+):** Establishment 24 forming

### RUSTY RICHARDS
**[CANON] - First Abductee Returns**
- No powers
- Had memories implanted by Grays (alien knowledge/warning)
- Only person abducted and returned

### THE GREAT SCRAMBLE
**[CANON] - Year 1, Month 3 onward**

Every government scrambles to identify their Living Super Weapons:
- **1.04-1.06:** First LSWs begin appearing publicly
- **1.06+:** THE SCRAMBLE accelerates
- Intelligence agencies tracking disappearances
- Military recruiting anyone "changed"
- Black market for LSW intelligence emerges
- Countries stealing LSWs from each other
- **Jackals** (bounty hunters) emerge targeting LSW biological samples
- LSWs forced into hiding to avoid conscription

### The Grays' Selection Method
**[CANON]**
- Abducted ~1,000 people worldwide (83-86 day cycles)
- Selected for psychological stability and moral character
- NOT selected for military skill or physical ability
- Result: Only ~2 "bad" LSW so far
- Most are good people but lack combat training
- Creates crisis: "You have godlike powers but no military training"

### Military Service Advantage
**[CANON] - Strategic World-Building**
- Countries with mandatory military service have advantage
- **Israel Example:** Mandatory IDF service means Israeli LSWs are already soldiers
  - Know tactics, discipline, chain of command
  - Israel punches above weight
- **USA/Other countries:** LSWs are random (teachers, musicians, accountants)
  - Have powers but no combat training
  - Must train civilian-powers from scratch

## YEAR 4: IRON EATERS ARRIVE (EARLY)

### Overview
**CRITICAL EVENT: Iron Eaters arrive 4 years EARLY.** Not expected until Year 8. This is the "unspoken allegiance" component of the Alliance of Four. Humanity makes first contact with one member of the invading forces. Panic spreads. Metal infrastructure begins vanishing. Charles Sapphire begins studying captured Iron Eater technology.

### Major Events

#### IRON EATER ARRIVAL
**[CANON] - Unspoken Allegiance Revealed**

**What Happens:**
- Iron Eaters arrive as dust/particles on crashed satellite
- NOT Year 8 as expected - they arrive 4 years EARLY
- This early arrival is their part in the Alliance of Four's plan
- Meant to weaken Earth's infrastructure before main invasion

**What They Do:**
- Nanite-based entities
- Mindless, just hungry machines
- Don't negotiate, don't talk
- Just consume metal
- Spread across planet
- Migrate seeking more metal sources
- Can detect metal across planets (like telescopes detecting elements)

**Impact:**
- Global infrastructure begins collapsing
- Power grids failing
- Communication networks damaged
- Transportation systems breaking down
- Apocalyptic atmosphere spreading
- Year of desperate measures

### Charles Sapphire's Response

#### ROBOFORGE DEVELOPMENT
**[CANON] - Reverse-Engineering Invasion**

**Timeline:**
- **Year 4:** Iron Eaters arrive
- **Year 4:** Sapphire studies captured Iron Eater technology/nanites
- **Year 4-5:** Develops RoboForge BASED ON Iron Eater principles
- Takes approximately 1 year to reverse-engineer
- "Slowly up the technology" - humanity doesn't have current LLM/AI capability
- RoboForge is adaptation of alien nanotech, not separate invention

**Why This Matters:**
- No "two different nanite technologies fighting each other"
- More realistic - humanity learns from the enemy
- Creates urgency - can only develop AFTER invasion starts
- Makes Sapphire's work feel earned, not convenient
